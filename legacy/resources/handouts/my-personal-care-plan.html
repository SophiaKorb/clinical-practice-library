Match support to friction

If care suddenly gets much harder, check health, pain, mood, sleep, and access with the right professional. Do not assume it is only a habit problem.

Make the routine workable

Plan around the person's priorities, access needs, and choice. Success may mean less effort or more control over the routine.

For a clinician or support person

  • Watch where the routine gets stuck. Offer one change at a time.
  • Ask before helping, touching, or changing products. Keep the person's privacy and choices central.
  • Keep helpful access supports available. Consider medical or occupational-therapy input for pain, motor demands, or persistent difficulty.
