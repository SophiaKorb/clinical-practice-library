Reading this comparison correctly

These are original short teaching summaries—not complete or verbatim DSM-5-TR criteria. A similar symptom such as poor attention does not imply similar disorders. Clinical diagnosis requires a complete history, relevant duration and exclusion checks, evidence of clinical significance, and professional judgment.

Formal diagnostic thresholds, specifiers and criteria should be checked against an authorized DSM-5-TR reference and current professional guidance. Clinical/editorial review of this prototype is pending.
