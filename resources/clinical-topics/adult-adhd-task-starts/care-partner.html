Caregiver, family or chosen supporter · Adults

The person decides whom they want involved whenever possible. Ask about privacy, preferred communication and practical help before using this guide. Your role is support and a clear handoff, not diagnosis or independent specialist treatment.

Ask about the bottleneck

Try: "What happens right before starting gets stuck?" Avoid laziness labels.

Help prepare by agreement

Offer one cue or materials support; do not take over all work.

Keep co-working consensual

Being alongside someone may help, but surveillance or pressure can worsen avoidance.

Support broader assessment

Relay patterns and context when invited; do not diagnose from a missed deadline.

A specific support agreement

Use fictional or appropriately de-identified notes. Entries stay in this open page; they are not saved or sent by this tool. Browser extensions or shared computers may still expose them. Clearing or leaving loses entries. Printing may include your notes.

Why the clinical formulation matters

Separate task comprehension, initiation, sequencing and completion. ADHD assessment requires developmental and cross-setting evidence and differential evaluation; a response to a timer or stimulant is not diagnostic proof. A support experiment can externalize the next action and reduce start cost without shaming the person. Look for competing explanations such as depression, sleep loss, pain, literacy demands and uncertainty about quality.

Discuss the formulation with the treating clinician when invited; do not use it to assign motives, override consent or change medication.

Safety and scope

Major sleep change, mood activation, severe depression, substance concerns or inability to meet basic needs needs clinical assessment. Do not advise medication changes or use task success as diagnostic proof.

Staff and care partners can support communication, observe and coordinate within their authorized role. Diagnosis, treatment selection and specialized interventions require appropriate training and supervision. This page is not a crisis service.
