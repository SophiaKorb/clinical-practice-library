Supervised reasoning practice · Adults

Fictional case: Pat wants to submit a reimbursement form but repeatedly opens email instead. The form requires finding receipts and interpreting ambiguous categories. A supervisor calls Pat unmotivated. Pat starts more easily when materials are ready and the first field is clarified. They also fear making a costly error. Choose a support experiment that keeps more than one explanation open.

What is the most useful next step, and why?

Choose an option to review the reasoning. This does not score clinical competence.

Worked formulation and supervision prompts

Separate task comprehension, initiation, sequencing and completion. ADHD assessment requires developmental and cross-setting evidence and differential evaluation; a response to a timer or stimulant is not diagnostic proof. A support experiment can externalize the next action and reduce start cost without shaming the person. Look for competing explanations such as depression, sleep loss, pain, literacy demands and uncertainty about quality.

What still needs assessment?

  • At the moment you try to begin, what is the next visible action?
  • What do you need to hold in mind, and what could be written or placed outside your memory?
  • What feels unclear, unpleasant or risky about starting?
  • When is the same task easier, and what is different then?

Explain which information could change your plan, which tasks your role permits, and who owns the next handoff. Ask a supervisor to review the reasoning and communication, rather than treating the preferred option as a universal answer.

Safety and scope

Major sleep change, mood activation, severe depression, substance concerns or inability to meet basic needs needs clinical assessment. Do not advise medication changes or use task success as diagnostic proof.

Staff and care partners can support communication, observe and coordinate within their authorized role. Diagnosis, treatment selection and specialized interventions require appropriate training and supervision. This page is not a crisis service.
