Caregiver, family or chosen supporter · Adults

The person decides whom they want involved whenever possible. Ask about privacy, preferred communication and practical help before using this guide. Your role is support and a clear handoff, not diagnosis or independent specialist treatment.

Stay warm

Try: "I know this doubt is painful. What did we agree would help you return to your day?"

Avoid joining endless proof searches

Follow a collaboratively agreed plan, while responding to real safety information.

Respect belief and privacy

Do not interrogate, ridicule or require disclosure of every intrusive thought.

Get specialist input

Support referral and supervision; family members do not independently design ERP.

A specific support agreement

Use fictional or appropriately de-identified notes. Entries stay in this open page; they are not saved or sent by this tool. Browser extensions or shared computers may still expose them. Clearing or leaving loses entries. Printing may include your notes.

Why the clinical formulation matters

Map the difference between ordinary reflection, a values-based practice and a repetitive attempt to obtain certainty or prevent a feared outcome. Relief after a ritual can strengthen repetition. Qualified assessment distinguishes OCD from other anxiety, depressive rumination, psychotic beliefs, trauma symptoms and actual risk. Do not dismiss a safety concern because the person also has OCD. Specialized ERP is collaborative and graded; surprise flooding or withholding all reassurance is not a treatment plan.

Discuss the formulation with the treating clinician when invited; do not use it to assign motives, override consent or change medication.

Safety and scope

Assess suicidality, harm intention, coercion and medical concerns on their own merits. Intrusive content is not automatically intent, but an OCD formulation does not rule out co-occurring danger. Immediate danger uses local urgent care.

Staff and care partners can support communication, observe and coordinate within their authorized role. Diagnosis, treatment selection and specialized interventions require appropriate training and supervision. This page is not a crisis service.
