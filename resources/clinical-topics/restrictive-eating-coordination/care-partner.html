Restrictive eating: medical coordination and supported participation — Care-partner guide

Clinician formulation guideInteractive case practiceVisual support mapClient workbookCare-partner guideProgress review tool

Caregiver, family or chosen supporter · Adults

The person decides whom they want involved whenever possible. Ask about privacy, preferred communication and practical help before using this guide. Your role is support and a clear handoff, not diagnosis or independent specialist treatment.

Avoid body comments

Focus on wellbeing and the agreed care plan, not appearance or praise for weight change.

Help access qualified care

Offer transport or help contacting the team with permission; relay dizziness/intake concerns promptly.

Follow the specialist plan

Do not independently set intake targets, force eating or monitor secretly.

Respect practical barriers

Help with agreed food access and cultural/sensory needs; do not interpret difficulty as defiance.

A specific support agreement

Use fictional or appropriately de-identified notes. Entries stay in this open page; they are not saved or sent by this tool. Browser extensions or shared computers may still expose them. Clearing or leaving loses entries. Printing may include your notes.

Why the clinical formulation matters

Keep medical/nutritional risk assessment distinct from psychological formulation. Fear of weight gain, sensory or fear-of-consequence avoidance, low appetite, gastrointestinal illness, food insecurity and medication effects can require different pathways. NG69 is not a complete ARFID pathway. This set helps coordinate qualified care and participation; it offers no calorie targets, weight thresholds, refeeding instructions or self-directed meal prescription.

Discuss the formulation with the treating clinician when invited; do not use it to assign motives, override consent or change medication.

Safety and scope

Fainting, severe weakness, dehydration, chest symptoms, rapid deterioration or other signs of medical instability require prompt medical assessment. Any body size can involve serious risk. Do not give refeeding or purging-management instructions from this tool.

Staff and care partners can support communication, observe and coordinate within their authorized role. Diagnosis, treatment selection and specialized interventions require appropriate training and supervision. This page is not a crisis service.
