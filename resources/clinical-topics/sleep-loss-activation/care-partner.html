Sleep loss: exhaustion or rising activation? — Care-partner guide

Clinician formulation guideInteractive case practiceVisual support mapClient workbookCare-partner guideProgress review tool

Caregiver, family or chosen supporter · Adults

The person decides whom they want involved whenever possible. Ask about privacy, preferred communication and practical help before using this guide. Your role is support and a clear handoff, not diagnosis or independent specialist treatment.

Ask about experience

Try: "Do you feel tired and unable to sleep, or unusually energized despite sleeping less?" Listen rather than assigning a label.

Help create opportunity

Offer practical help with noise, transport or caregiving if wanted. Sleep advice cannot replace safe housing or time to rest.

Notice clustering

Describe new changes in mood, decisions and activity alongside sleep; avoid surveillance or policing every bedtime.

Follow the agreed contact plan

Help the person access assessment when changes accelerate or safety is affected. Do not provide sedatives or medication advice.

A specific support agreement

Use fictional or appropriately de-identified notes. Entries stay in this open page; they are not saved or sent by this tool. Browser extensions or shared computers may still expose them. Clearing or leaving loses entries. Printing may include your notes.

Why the clinical formulation matters

Assess sleep opportunity, reported sleep, daytime sleepiness and energy separately. A change toward reduced need for sleep with elevated or irritable mood and increased activity can indicate an episode requiring prompt evaluation. Insomnia treatment is not automatically suitable during unstable mood, acute psychosis or dangerous sleepiness. This tool helps organize a handoff; it must not become a self-triage test or a schedule for restricting sleep.

Discuss the formulation with the treating clinician when invited; do not use it to assign motives, override consent or change medication.

Safety and scope

Rapid activation with low sleep, acute confusion, dangerous sleepiness, suicidal thinking or inability to function requires timely clinical assessment. Avoid driving or hazardous tasks when unsafe; use local urgent help for immediate danger.

Staff and care partners can support communication, observe and coordinate within their authorized role. Diagnosis, treatment selection and specialized interventions require appropriate training and supervision. This page is not a crisis service.
