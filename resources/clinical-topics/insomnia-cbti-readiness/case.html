Worked formulation and supervision prompts

Multicomponent CBT-I is a structured evidence-based treatment for suitable chronic insomnia presentations; sleep hygiene advice alone is not equivalent. Treatment components need qualified selection, adaptation and monitoring. This aid organizes referral and access questions, not instructions to restrict sleep or modify medication. Evaluate sleepiness, driving/hazardous work, unstable mood, seizure/other medical considerations and feasibility with the treating clinician.

What still needs assessment?

  • When do you have an actual chance to sleep, and what gets in the way?
  • How does the sleep problem affect your day, especially safety-sensitive tasks?
  • What health, mood, medication or breathing changes should be assessed?
  • What would make structured sleep treatment possible in your life?

Explain which information could change your plan, which tasks your role permits, and who owns the next handoff. Ask a supervisor to review the reasoning and communication, rather than treating the preferred option as a universal answer.

Safety and scope

Dangerous sleepiness, rapid mood activation, acute confusion or new medical concerns require timely assessment. Do not drive or perform hazardous tasks when unsafe. Sleep restriction and medication changes require qualified care.

Staff and care partners can support communication, observe and coordinate within their authorized role. Diagnosis, treatment selection and specialized interventions require appropriate training and supervision. This page is not a crisis service.
