How tags and related resources work

Discovery tags are generated from resource titles and paths, with manually prioritized flagship tools and role corrections for original guides. New topic resources have curated audience, task and pathway metadata; topic tags still use titles and paths. Tags are not clinical endorsement, proven indications, age validation, or permission to practice. Related links share topic and pathway metadata; review the actual resource before using it. Sources, restrictions, and clinical limits are stated within each guide. Filters combine; clear one if results disappear. Untagged resources remain searchable.

Search and filters can be shared through the URL. Do not include client names or identifying details in searches or shared links.
