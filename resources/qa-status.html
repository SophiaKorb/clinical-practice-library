About these resources

The Clinical Practice Library brings together educational guides, worksheets, visual tools, and links to outside services. Choose the resource that fits the person, their goals, their age, and the setting in which it will be used.

How to use a guide or worksheet

Check who it is written for and whether it requires a trained clinician or other professional. Many CPL worksheets are original teaching aids, not independently validated assessment measures or treatment protocols. They do not replace an individualized evaluation, specialist care, or urgent safety assessment.

Checking current information

Clinical guidance and community programs can change. Consult linked primary sources and confirm a service's availability, eligibility, and contact information with the provider before relying on it. A resource appearing in the library is not an endorsement that it suits every person.

Reading, printing, and sharing

Use the web version for interactive tools. Printed copies and browser-created PDFs may have different navigation or screen-reader behavior. Check readability and accessibility before sharing, and do not enter identifying client information into public tools.
