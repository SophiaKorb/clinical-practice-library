Special education due process and dispute resolution

The District of Columbia's Office of Dispute Resolution provides procedures for IDEA due process complaints, including issues involving identification, evaluation, educational placement, and FAPE.

For questions about eligibility, services, or the plan, start by requesting a meeting and the applicable parent-rights information. Keep copies of school communications and decisions. Official state resources below explain additional options if a disagreement remains.

Educational guidance only, not legal advice or an official state publication. Confirm current procedures with the relevant education agency. Federal and state remedies may have different standards and deadlines.

Getting started with school support

  1. Write down the student's strengths, specific difficulties, and examples from school.
  2. Ask the receiving school or district about special education referral and evaluation steps.
  3. Keep dated copies of written requests, school notices, evaluation documents, and replies.
  4. Request current procedural safeguards and clarify how the student and family participate in decisions.

DC families can consult OSSE resources on special education, parent participation, and due process.

Getting ready for the meeting

Gather evaluations, attendance and work records, family observations, student priorities, and questions about goals, instruction, accommodations, related services, transportation where relevant, and progress reporting. Ask for a copy of the plan and confirm next steps in writing.

If your child changes schools or states

Send the new district the most recent IEP and evaluations promptly. Ask the team about comparable services during the transition, the timing of an appropriate review, and local contacts. The new jurisdiction's procedures may differ.
