Two blank sheets for learning disorders

Working sheet 01 — what matters

Learning disorders: my experience

These prompts are choices, not required questions.

1. What I want to read, calculate, write or communicate
2. What step gets difficult and under what conditions
3. What I can demonstrate in a different format
4. What teaching or support has helped before
5. What accessibility change I would choose

Working sheet 02 — a chosen next step

Learning disorders: support and review

A pause or decision not to proceed can be the right next step.

1. A specific skill question for further evaluation
2. Immediate access support and who provides it
3. Instructional response and progress-monitoring approach
4. How I would like to show growth
5. What to revisit if the support is not helping
