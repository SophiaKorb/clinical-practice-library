What question should we explore first?

Only a topic selection is made; no personal text is entered, stored or sent.

Word reading

What might be happening?

Decoding, spelling, word recognition and fluency

What needs distinguishing?

What changes when text is read aloud or presented in an accessible format? Separate decoding from comprehension.

A possible next connection

Ask for a skill-specific literacy assessment when indicated; provide explicit, systematic reading instruction and access supports.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Mathematics

What might be happening?

Number magnitude, math language, procedures and reasoning

What needs distinguishing?

Is number understanding difficult, or does reading/working memory obscure mathematical knowledge? What teaching has been available?

A possible next connection

Use systematic teaching, representations, number lines and monitored math supports as indicated; clarify the actual assessed skill.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Written expression

What might be happening?

Transcription, sentence construction, spelling and ideas

What needs distinguishing?

Compare oral language, handwriting, spelling, typing and extended composition separately.

A possible next connection

Offer explicit writing strategy instruction and accessible output modes; do not treat neatness as the quality of thought.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Task access

What might be happening?

Language, instruction, sensory demands and timing

What needs distinguishing?

Is the task inaccessible because of environment, disability, teaching opportunity, language mismatch or directions?

A possible next connection

Adjust access immediately and investigate learning needs through properly authorized evaluation; supports need not wait for diagnosis.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Follow through

Choose a blank two-page working sheet if helpful; never place identifying clinical details in a public site's search or URL.
