Practice labs / Psychosis / bipolar / Printable worksheet

Adults and qualified mental-health clinicians; chosen support people with consent

Two blank sheets for psychosis / bipolar

Use spoken language, drawings, supported communication or paper writing. Skip any item and handle any completed sheets under applicable privacy policy.

Facilitator guideVisual map

Working sheet 01 — what matters

Psychosis / bipolar: my experience

These prompts are choices, not required questions.

1. What changed from my usual baseline and when
2. Sleep need, mood, energy and functioning over time
3. What I noticed versus what another person observed
4. Medication, physical-health or substance questions to review
5. What is uncertain and needs qualified assessment

Working sheet 02 — a chosen next step

Psychosis / bipolar: support and review

A pause or decision not to proceed can be the right next step.

1. What currently matters most to me
2. Experiences I want help coping with, in my words
3. One recovery or participation goal I choose
4. What I authorize the care team to discuss or share
5. When and how I want support reviewed
