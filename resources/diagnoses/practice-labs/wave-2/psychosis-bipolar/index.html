Episodes and present-day recovery: two different maps

A clinical chronology of mood, sleep, unusual experiences and functioning is different from a person-led map of distress, daily participation and chosen supports. Use both without turning the diagram into a diagnosis.

Start with the person's question. These tools do not establish diagnoses, prescribe treatment, determine safety or replace appropriate professional assessment. People may decline any activity. No client details should be entered into public search or URLs.
