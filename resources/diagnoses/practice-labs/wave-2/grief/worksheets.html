Two blank sheets for grief / bereavement

Use spoken language, drawings, supported communication or paper writing. Skip any item and handle any completed sheets under applicable privacy policy.

Working sheet 01 — what matters

Grief / bereavement: my experience

These prompts are choices, not required questions.

1. What or whom I want to remember today
2. What kinds of support I welcome—and what I do not
3. A memory, ritual or form of connection I may choose
4. Who may share this with me or give me privacy
5. What I would like people to stop assuming

Working sheet 02 — a chosen next step

Grief / bereavement: support and review

A pause or decision not to proceed can be the right next step.

1. One everyday responsibility I need help with
2. A support person or accommodation I would choose
3. My cultural, family or spiritual preferences and boundaries
4. How I would know this support helps
5. Whether I want another conversation or specialist support
