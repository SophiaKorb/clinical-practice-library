Adults, authorized support staff and qualified SUD clinicians

Use this material collaboratively

A medical safety issue, a person-led goal, a treatment-access question and a privacy concern are different starting points. Support reflection without assigning an abstinence goal or a readiness stage.

Keep professional roles clear. Medical or diagnostic decisions, specialist therapies, safeguarding, consent and eligibility rules are handled through the appropriate qualified pathway. These exercises are optional visual supports.

Seven moves for a session

  1. Ask permission to discuss substance use, and offer the person a genuine option to decline.
  2. Prioritize immediate medical and safety assessment if overdose, withdrawal or severe impairment is suspected.
  3. Explore the person's own values, preferred outcomes and ambivalence with open questions.
  4. Separate medical treatment from psychosocial support, safer-environment strategies and practical access.
  5. Offer evidence-informed options without mandating abstinence or a readiness label.
  6. Clarify consent, record access, program requirements and care-team roles before information is shared.
  7. Choose one safe next connection and review dignity, safety and meaningful function.

Distinctions that change the plan

Motivational interviewing is collaborative, not a technique for winning compliance.

Severe withdrawal, particularly alcohol/benzodiazepine withdrawal, requires medical evaluation; this is not a detox guide.

Medication options differ by disorder and require qualified prescribing and individualized care.

Some SUD treatment records have additional 42 CFR Part 2 protections; do not assume permission from a general release.

Illustrative fictional example

A fictional adult wants fewer missed workdays and worries that stopping alcohol abruptly could be unsafe. The clinician offers medical withdrawal assessment, honors choice, and checks confidentiality before coordinating follow-up. A self-directed detox date is not planned on the page.

The scenario is invented and not a claim of effectiveness. Ask which assumptions the person might challenge.
