Adults, authorized support staff and qualified SUD clinicians

Safety, choice, treatment and privacy: distinct next steps

A medical safety issue, a person-led goal, a treatment-access question and a privacy concern are different starting points. Support reflection without assigning an abstinence goal or a readiness stage.

Start with the person's question. These tools do not establish diagnoses, prescribe treatment, determine safety or replace appropriate professional assessment. People may decline any activity. No client details should be entered into public search or URLs.
