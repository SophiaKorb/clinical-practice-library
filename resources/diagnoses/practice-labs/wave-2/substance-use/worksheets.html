Practice labs / Substance use / Printable worksheet

Adults, authorized support staff and qualified SUD clinicians

Two blank sheets for substance use

Use spoken language, drawings, supported communication or paper writing. Skip any item and handle any completed sheets under applicable privacy policy.

Facilitator guideVisual map

Working sheet 01 — what matters

Substance use: my experience

These prompts are choices, not required questions.

1. What I want from this conversation today
2. Health or safety questions to ask a clinician
3. What feels useful and what has become costly
4. Which care or practical options I want to hear about
5. My privacy concerns and consent preferences

Working sheet 02 — a chosen next step

Substance use: support and review

A pause or decision not to proceed can be the right next step.

1. My next safe and chosen connection
2. Who can assist within their role
3. Medical decisions this worksheet cannot make
4. What information may be shared, with whom and how
5. What useful follow-up would feel like to me
