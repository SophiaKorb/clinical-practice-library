What question should we explore first?

Only a topic selection is made; no personal text is entered, stored or sent.

Medical safety

What might be happening?

Overdose, intoxication or withdrawal concern

What needs distinguishing?

Is there suspected overdose, severe intoxication, dangerous withdrawal or medical instability? Alcohol/benzodiazepine withdrawal can be serious.

A possible next connection

Use urgent/emergency or qualified medical evaluation. Do not use a worksheet to plan abrupt cessation, detox or medication dosing.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Personal goals

What might be happening?

What the person wants and values

What needs distinguishing?

What feels helpful, costly, complicated or worth changing? Is the person willing to discuss options?

A possible next connection

Offer a collaborative motivational conversation and a chosen next practical support without coercion.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Care options

What might be happening?

Counseling, medication and coordinated services

What needs distinguishing?

Which substance(s), co-occurring conditions and preferences shape indicated treatment? Is medication treatment for OUD or AUD relevant?

A possible next connection

Offer accurate local pathways and authorized warm handoffs; prescribing and diagnosis remain qualified professional roles.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Confidentiality

What might be happening?

Consent and who learns what

What needs distinguishing?

Is the information covered by additional SUD-record protections? Who is seeking disclosure and what is authorized?

A possible next connection

Confirm applicability of 42 CFR Part 2 and other rules with organizational privacy professionals; do not enter client details here.

Discuss with the person. The choices are examples, not individual clinical recommendations.

Follow through

Choose a blank two-page working sheet if helpful; never place identifying clinical details in a public site's search or URL.
