Practice labs for shared clinical thinking

Four distinct printable tools for turning formulation into person-chosen, concrete next steps. Every lab includes a visual structure, clinical distinctions, a fictional example, facilitation guidance and two large-print blank worksheets.

Scope matters. These are original educational aids, not validated measures or therapy protocols. The maps are not interchangeable between diagnoses. Clinical, legal, privacy, developmental, native accessibility and release review are pending.

Anxiety / OCD

Separate generalized worry, panic, social fears and obsessive-compulsive rituals before choosing a treatment model.

Open Anxiety and OCD: map the maintaining loop

Depression

Plan meaningful action without mistaking depleted energy, disability or social constraints for insufficient motivation.

Open Depression: capacity, meaning and activity

Trauma / dissociation

Offer choice about orientation, safety and continuity without requiring memory retrieval, disclosure or compulsory grounding.

Open Trauma and dissociation: choice and continuity

Autism

Treat access needs and communication as information. Design the environment with the person before asking for more adaptation from them.

Open Autism: change the access conditions

How these fit into the CPL

Begin with the existing clinical-depth guides for all fourteen discovery categories. Use a practice lab when a person and clinician want a blank, optional shared-thinking structure. Check the role and specialist boundaries on each page. ADHD already has its own larger collection and case-management toolkit; these labs do not replace it.
