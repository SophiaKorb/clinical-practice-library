    What “all resources” means here

    This index includes all materials with the selected diagnosis tag in the current CPL finder, not an evidence-certified treatment library or a list of every possible clinical support. Some general-function resources are not diagnosis-tagged; select “Related skills” or search the full 615-item catalog. Published external resource links should be checked with their providers for current eligibility and accuracy. Clinical judgment, rights, developmental suitability and specialist training still matter.
