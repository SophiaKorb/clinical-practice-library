Case management across clinical conditions

Fourteen condition-aware working sheets. Each provides specific questions to explore, actions case managers can coordinate, a person-led visual route, a blank support worksheet, and a clear boundary for qualified clinical assessment.

Use the right level of supportCase management includes practical service navigation, advocacy, accessibility adaptations and authorized coordination. It does not replace psychotherapy, diagnosis, medication management, acute clinical assessment or determination of legal eligibility. Do not treat a diagnosis label as evidence that any particular support is required.

Evidence and boundaries

AHRQ care coordination and AHRQ shared-care planning inform the role-level organization. Each clinical-depth guide links to its condition-specific source evidence. These worksheets are original educational aids without independent treatment validation.

Age, guardian or Part 2 consent, actual eligibility, agency role, clinical urgency, communication access and safeguarding must be reviewed for each individual. No PHI, no merge, no deployment, and no rights/publication authorization.
