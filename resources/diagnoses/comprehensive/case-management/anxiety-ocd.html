Anxiety and OCD: access without reinforcing rituals

Use practical supports to improve access, choice and participation. Case management does not diagnose a condition or replace specialized clinical care.

Case management boundariesSupport resource navigation, skill rehearsal, authorized care coordination and advocacy according to organizational scope and consent. Verify actual referral eligibility, safety and privacy rules. Do not mistake a checklist for clinical treatment.

Six things to do together

  1. Clarify whether the service barrier is worry, avoidance, panic, compulsions, sensory difficulty or another concern, without diagnosing.
  2. Support appointment access and practice neutral communication with providers rather than repeatedly providing certainty about feared outcomes.
  3. For OCD, coordinate referral to trained ERP clinicians and avoid inventing reassurance or exposure tasks as case-management treatment.
  4. Help the person choose one safe logistics action and track whether it improves access without escalating demands.
  5. Review participation, cost, distress and consent to further coordination.

When to coordinate qualified care

Acute risk, medical causes of panic symptoms, severe impairment or treatment destabilization warrant qualified assessment.

Review the clinical-depth guide, differential and linked evidence before selecting clinical treatment.
