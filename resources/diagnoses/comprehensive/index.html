Four condition-specific practice labs

New additive review material: original visual formulation structures, clinician-supported exercises and two blank printable worksheets per topic. These supplements do not replace specialist therapies or the fourteen full clinical-depth guides.

  • Anxiety and OCD: map the maintaining loop — Separate generalized worry, panic, social fears and obsessive-compulsive rituals before choosing a treatment model.
  • Depression: capacity, meaning and activity — Plan meaningful action without mistaking depleted energy, disability or social constraints for insufficient motivation.
  • Trauma and dissociation: choice and continuity — Offer choice about orientation, safety and continuity without requiring memory retrieval, disclosure or compulsory grounding.
  • Autism: change the access conditions — Treat access needs and communication as information. Design the environment with the person before asking for more adaptation from them.

Evidence and release boundaries

These are original educational draft guides grounded in linked primary-source guidelines. They are not individually validated diagnostic or treatment instruments, do not authorize prescribing or specialized treatment, and do not substitute for legal, medical or clinical review. Age and care-setting differences must be assessed for actual use.
