My morning launch map

Choose one point in the morning that could be easier. We are designing a path, not testing obedience.

Young person's choice comes first. The person may speak, type, point, draw or use another communication form. Do not treat this as a test, disciplinary contract, or substitute for a safety/clinical process.

Build a shared plan

1. The night-before helper
2. The getting-ready step
3. The leaving-the-house cue
  1. Set out only the things the young person agrees are useful: clothes, backpack, lunch items or a written plan.
  2. Choose a visual or spoken cue for the one step that usually fails; check sensory, sleep and transport needs.
  3. Mark what 'ready enough' means and who has authority to change the plan when things go wrong.
