ADHD support: three working sheets

Print only the pages you want to use. Use the client's language and choices. Do not place PHI in public or shared tools.

1 · Understand the barrier

What does the client want to do?___________________________________________________________________
What is already working?___________________________________________________________________
What becomes difficult (start, sequence, remember, switch, time, access or emotion)?___________________________________________________________________
Which situation or environmental factor makes it harder?___________________________________________________________________
Which possible explanation needs more information?___________________________________________________________________

2 · Agree on one action

Which support does the client choose?___________________________________________________________________
What is the first physical or communication action?___________________________________________________________________
What will the client do, if anything?___________________________________________________________________
What will the case manager do?___________________________________________________________________
Who else may be contacted, with what consent?___________________________________________________________________
When will we check back, and by which safe method?___________________________________________________________________

3 · Review without blame

What happened since last time?___________________________________________________________________
Did the chosen action improve the desired outcome?___________________________________________________________________
What did it cost in time, money, stress or extra help?___________________________________________________________________
Does the client want to keep, change or stop the support?___________________________________________________________________
Is there a new service, safety or eligibility question to escalate?___________________________________________________________________

Not a diagnostic scale, legally valid service plan or clinical assessment.
