My voice at a team meeting

The team needs to hear what matters to the young person, not only what adults think should change.

Young person's choice comes first. The person may speak, type, point, draw or use another communication form. Do not treat this as a test, disciplinary contract, or substitute for a safety/clinical process.

Build a shared plan

1. Choose what I want to say
2. Choose how I will participate
3. Check what adults agreed
  1. Offer speaking, writing, drawing, AAC, an advocate or advance questions, with appropriate consent and privacy.
  2. Practice one request about safety, school access, strengths or a goal; invite the young person to reject staff-created goals.
  3. After the meeting, share a short accessible summary and ask the young person to correct what adults misunderstood.
