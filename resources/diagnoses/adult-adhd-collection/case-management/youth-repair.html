When something goes wrong: safety and repair

Accountability is different from humiliation. Focus on safety, impact, making amends and prevention.

Young person's choice comes first. The person may speak, type, point, draw or use another communication form. Do not treat this as a test, disciplinary contract, or substitute for a safety/clinical process.

Build a shared plan

1. Make sure everyone is safe
2. Understand impact and choices
3. Choose repair and a better support
  1. Prioritize immediate safety and separate people if needed. Serious threats or harm require established clinical/safeguarding procedures.
  2. When calm enough, help the youth describe observable events, feelings and effects without forced confession or leading questions.
  3. Explore a voluntary, proportionate repair and a change to access or supervision that reduces recurrence; honor the harmed person's needs.
