Employment, education and accommodations

I want to participate at work or school with supports that respect my choices.

Start with the client's choice. This activity supports practical planning and coordination. It is not a diagnostic assessment, medication protocol or psychotherapy. Ask permission before contacting another person or service.

What may be making this difficult?

  • Unwritten priorities, interruptions, sensory load and multiple bosses can create task failures.
  • The person may not wish to disclose a diagnosis, and legal eligibility is individualized.
  • An accommodation request is a process, not an automatic entitlement or a guaranteed outcome.

These are hypotheses to explore, not explanations to impose. Ask what is already working.

Use this in a case-management visit

  1. Ask what task or barrier matters at work/school; identify performance expectation and present environmental obstacle.
  2. Try a neutral access request: written priorities, quieter space, predictable check-ins or task demonstration.
  3. Practice an optional script describing functional need and requested adjustment without requiring diagnostic disclosure.
  4. With consent, identify the correct HR, supervisor, disability-services or school contact, and verify requirements.
  5. Set a follow-up: what adjustment was attempted, what the client experienced, what to do if denied.

Follow-through and documentation

Coordination boundary: Do not promise ADA, Section 504, IEP, vocational rehabilitation, disability benefits or employment eligibility; verify current local criteria and referral channels.

What to measure: Meaningful participation, tolerable workload, autonomy and accommodation usefulness.

Ask the client: Did this make the situation easier? What did it cost you? Do you want to keep, change or discard this support?

Practice basis and limitations

The practical workflows here are original educational case-management aids, not separately validated ADHD treatments. They emphasize person-centered coordination and environmental adaptations informed by NICE NG87, AHRQ shared care planning, and AHRQ care coordination. For workplace examples see the Job Accommodation Network. Verify all program criteria and laws before making referrals.

Reviewed for drafting November 17, 2026. Open gates: clinician and case-management supervision, privacy, accessibility, rights, local agency policy, physical printing and release approval. Never record PHI in public tools.
