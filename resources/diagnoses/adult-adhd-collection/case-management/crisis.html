When the plan breaks down: repair and escalation

I want a way back into the plan after setbacks, without punishment.

Start with the client's choice. This activity supports practical planning and coordination. It is not a diagnostic assessment, medication protocol or psychotherapy. Ask permission before contacting another person or service.
UnderstandDo togetherPrint worksheetReview

What may be making this difficult?

  • A missed step triggers shame or avoidance of future contact.
  • Immediate needs, self-neglect, housing risk, suicidal thoughts or severe mood symptoms require more than organization coaching.
  • Multiple agencies may assume someone else is coordinating.

These are hypotheses to explore, not explanations to impose. Ask what is already working.

Use this in a case-management visit

  1. Ask what went wrong and what is most urgent, using the person's words and no blame.
  2. Sort: urgent safety/medical need; resource access; administrative repair; skill experiment. Escalate immediately under agency protocol where indicated.
  3. For ordinary setbacks, create the next smallest repair step and safe re-entry contact plan.
  4. For urgent needs, connect to qualified clinical/crisis services and follow current agency pathways; document handoff and follow-through.
  5. Review whether the support system itself was too complicated or inaccessible, and simplify it.

Follow-through and documentation

Coordination boundary: New suicidality, marked activation, psychosis, intoxication/withdrawal, abuse or inability to meet basic needs warrant qualified urgent assessment; do not substitute a productivity plan.

What to measure: Timely safety handoff when indicated; re-entry into chosen services; reduced support burden.

Ask the client: Did this make the situation easier? What did it cost you? Do you want to keep, change or discard this support?

Practice basis and limitations

The practical workflows here are original educational case-management aids, not separately validated ADHD treatments. They emphasize person-centered coordination and environmental adaptations informed by NICE NG87, AHRQ shared care planning, and AHRQ care coordination. For workplace examples see the Job Accommodation Network. Verify all program criteria and laws before making referrals.

Use within your professional role and supervision requirements. Check consent, privacy, accessibility, rights, local agency policy, and current eligibility. Do not enter identifying client information into public tools.
