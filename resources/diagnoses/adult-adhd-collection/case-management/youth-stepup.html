My supported independence ladder

Independence grows through meaningful choice and rehearsal with supports—not by suddenly removing help.

Young person's choice comes first. The person may speak, type, point, draw or use another communication form. Do not treat this as a test, disciplinary contract, or substitute for a safety/clinical process.

Build a shared plan

1. Choose a skill I want
2. Practice with support I select
3. Review and adjust support
  1. Choose a concrete skill such as checking appointments, making a call, planning transport or preparing documents.
  2. Agree on a level of support: model together, do side by side, have a cue nearby, or try independently with a recovery plan.
  3. Decrease support only by shared decision and based on safety and demonstrated comfort, not arbitrary age expectations.
