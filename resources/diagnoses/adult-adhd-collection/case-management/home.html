Home routines, food and personal care

I want routines that fit the life I actually live.

Start with the client's choice. This activity supports practical planning and coordination. It is not a diagnostic assessment, medication protocol or psychotherapy. Ask permission before contacting another person or service.
UnderstandDo togetherPrint worksheetReview

What may be making this difficult?

  • Routines require many transitions: notice need, gather supplies, initiate, complete and reset.
  • Sensory experience, money, food access, fatigue, pain and depression may be more important than attention.
  • A perfect schedule may cost more energy than the routine is worth.

These are hypotheses to explore, not explanations to impose. Ask what is already working.

Use this in a case-management visit

  1. Choose one routine the client wants to improve, such as laundry, meals, dishes, bathing or medication pickup.
  2. Map 'before/during/after' and identify which one step fails most often.
  3. Offer point-of-use storage, simpler sequence, duplicate supplies, low-energy option and a chosen cue.
  4. If basic needs are unmet, assess immediate resource access and coordinate eligible services instead of prescribing a habit.
  5. Practice a realistic version; review privacy, safety and client preferences.

Follow-through and documentation

Coordination boundary: Pain, eating restriction, severe self-neglect or inability to meet basic needs requires qualified assessment and possible urgent coordination.

What to measure: Participation, safety, dignity, sensory comfort, sustainability and burden.

Ask the client: Did this make the situation easier? What did it cost you? Do you want to keep, change or discard this support?

Practice basis and limitations

The practical workflows here are original educational case-management aids, not separately validated ADHD treatments. They emphasize person-centered coordination and environmental adaptations informed by NICE NG87, AHRQ shared care planning, and AHRQ care coordination. For workplace examples see the Job Accommodation Network. Verify all program criteria and laws before making referrals.
