My change-of-activity plan

Switching tasks can be difficult even when both activities are good. Build a predictable, chosen transition.

Young person's choice comes first. The person may speak, type, point, draw or use another communication form. Do not treat this as a test, disciplinary contract, or substitute for a safety/clinical process.

Build a shared plan

1. Know what is next
2. End the first activity
3. Begin the next one
  1. Show the next activity with a calendar, picture, words or object cue; ask what warning feels useful.
  2. Choose an ending ritual: save work, put supplies down, take a pause or signal the next person.
  3. Make the first next step simple, and build a return plan when the transition does not go smoothly.
