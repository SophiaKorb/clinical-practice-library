Use one consistent clinical route

  1. SeeingWhat is reported, observed and affecting function?
  2. ExplainingWhich competing/co-occurring explanations remain?
  3. AssessingWhat evidence, access or collateral would change the plan?
  4. DoingWhich intervention, coordination or referral fits the person's goals and clinician competence?
  5. UsingWhat practical accessible tool supports action?
  6. RevisitingWhat changed, what did not, what was harmful, and when must we reformulate?

This is a non-linear thinking aid: urgent actions can occur at any point, and new evidence sends the clinician back to earlier questions.
