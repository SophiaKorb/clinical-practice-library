Original observation tool · Psychosis and mood

One timeline, four kinds of information

Use for a non-leading discussion of change over time and support needs. It cannot diagnose bipolar disorder, psychosis or a medical condition; it does not predict dangerousness or require agreement about the cause of unusual experiences.

Do not put identifying health information into a shared browser page. CPL does not save or submit entries; local browser features and printouts may expose them. Acute safety, severe mental-state or new medical changes require appropriate qualified care.

Clinical boundaries

Questions about acute safety, potential mania/psychosis, new neurological issues, possible severe adverse medication effects or substance withdrawal may need urgent clinical evaluation, not this tool. There are no diagnostic thresholds or algorithms. Specialty supervision and collaborative consent remain necessary.
