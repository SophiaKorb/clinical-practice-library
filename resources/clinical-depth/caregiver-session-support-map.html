Original practical tool · Child and caregiver learning

One activity. One support. One shared review.

A visual coaching map for a safe everyday routine. Use pointing, drawing, speaking or writing. This is not an official PCIT, CPP, child-centered play therapy or behavior protocol. Never use it to override a child's safety, assent, accessibility or basic needs.

Use fictional or de-identified examples if working in a web browser. Nothing is sent to CPL, but entries remain visible until cleared and may appear in print. Do not include identifying clinical data or leave a completed worksheet on a shared device.

Before → During → After shows a planning cycle, not that one adult intervention guarantees a specific behavior. If safety concerns emerge, use appropriate protective procedures rather than continuing the exercise.

Plan an actual practice attempt

Review more than compliance

What actually happened?

Note child safety and comfort, independent or supported participation, emotional recovery, communication and choice. Record what you observed rather than assuming what a child intended.

How much did it cost?

Note caregiver workload, loss of choice, unintended distress, reinforcement mismatch, environmental barriers and which supports are sustainable.

Clinical boundaries

Behavior may reflect communication, pain, sensory demands, developmental skills, learning access, trauma, fear, relationships or several factors. This original map is for caregiver education/observation, not formal functional behavior assessment, child protection, medical evaluation or specialist therapy fidelity. If danger, abuse or severe developmental/medical regression is suspected, assess and refer appropriately.

Clinician explanation: Child/caregiver treatment-selection guide. Official orientation: NCTSN PCIT and NCTSN early-childhood interventions.
