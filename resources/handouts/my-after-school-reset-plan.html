For adults: offer choices without making this a quiz. Plan during a calm time. The child can tell or show when they have more room; address immediate safety needs calmly.

Plan the landing before school ends

Notice patterns across school, travel, and home. A reset is support, not something a child must earn.

For a clinician or support person

  • Offer time, space, and a manageable way to communicate after overload.
  • Ask which choices help. Keep touch, noise, and talking within what the child can manage.
  • Share useful observations with school or a clinician when needed. This plan does not explain every cause of distress.
