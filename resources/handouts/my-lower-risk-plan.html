A change I want to consider

Plan for a difficult moment

A plan reduces risk; it does not remove it. A remote check-in cannot provide immediate hands-on help. Drug checking has limits. Naloxone treats opioid overdose; emergency care is still needed. Discuss medication treatment, safer supplies, and withdrawal care with appropriate services.

Show a short example

Risk: opioids may be involved and nobody nearby knows. Step: avoid being alone, obtain naloxone, and make sure someone present knows where it is and how to respond.
