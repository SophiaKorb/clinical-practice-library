Sibling Conflict Reset

Created by Sophia Cohon, PhD

A short reflection and repair plan for ordinary sibling conflict

This sheet is for ordinary sibling conflict after everyone is safe enough to think. It is not a joint worksheet for situations involving fear, coercion, serious aggression, threats, sexual behavior, or abuse.

If You Are Doing This With an Adult

The adult’s job is to support safety and problem-solving, not to force agreement or decide that both children are equally responsible.

If one sibling is afraid of the other, use separate support and get additional help rather than requiring a joint repair conversation.
