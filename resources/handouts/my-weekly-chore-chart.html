If a Job Keeps Getting Missed

A missed box is information, not a character grade. Check whether the job is clear, realistic, physically and sensory-accessible, and easy enough to remember and start. Change the task or support when needed.

For Parents and Caregivers

Use this chart as a memory and routine support, not as surveillance. Teach new chores first, define what “done” means, and give more support when a task is new or difficult. Gradually reduce help as the child becomes more independent.

This chart works well with the Chore Tips and My Chore Plan resources when a family needs more help figuring out why a task is difficult.
