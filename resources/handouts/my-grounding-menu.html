External Orientation

☐ Name the date, place, and what I am doing
☐ Describe five neutral things I can see
☐ Read signs, labels, or a short paragraph
☐ Listen for three separate sounds
☐ Name what is different from the memory or fear

Touch and Movement

☐ Feet on the floor
☐ Notice the chair or wall supporting me
☐ Hold an object and describe its texture or weight
☐ Stretch or change position
☐ Walk and notice each step

Thinking Tasks

☐ Count objects
☐ Name items in a category
☐ Spell a familiar word backward
☐ Describe the next three steps of a routine
☐ Do a simple mental task that is absorbing but not frustrating

Connection

☐ Hear a trusted person’s voice
☐ Text or call someone
☐ Sit near another person without needing to talk
☐ Use a familiar object, photo, pet, or place
