When a grief wave hits

If safety or basic care is becoming difficult, bring that concern to a clinician. This map is a conversation aid, not a safety assessment.

Make room for grief, together

Ask which part of the map the person wants to talk about. Leave space for mixed feelings, silence, and ordinary life.

For a clinician or support person

  • Use the person's own words. Do not require a positive meaning or a particular grief stage.
  • Offer practical help and company in the form the person wants.
  • Ask about changes in daily functioning and safety when concerns arise; use a fuller assessment when needed.
