For Parents and Caregivers

  • Offer only a few relevant choices when the full board is too much.
  • Accept pointing, reaching, signing, AAC, words, or another clear communication method.
  • Do not use the board as a test. The child does not have to point correctly, make eye contact, or say the word aloud before you respond.
  • If the child communicates pain, danger, illness, or another urgent need, address that first.

Keep the Board Usable

Practice during calm routines so the choices are familiar. Remove options that are confusing and add words, pictures, or symbols the child already uses. The goal is functional communication, not perfect labeling.
