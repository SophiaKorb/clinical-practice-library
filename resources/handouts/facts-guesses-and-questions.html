For Adults and Clinicians

Do not grade the learner on selecting the adult's preferred explanation. Look for differentiation among fact, inference, uncertainty, and action. Include the learner's own perspective rather than treating it as an obstacle. Do not use cognitive flexibility to override credible safety or boundary concerns.
