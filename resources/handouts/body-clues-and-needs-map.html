Body Clues and Needs Map

Created by Sophia Cohon, PhD

You can start with what your body is doing. You do not need the perfect feeling word.

1. Something Changed

☐ A little   ☐ A lot   ☐ I am not sure

2. My Energy

☐ Very low   ☐ Low   ☐ About right   ☐ High   ☐ Very high   ☐ It keeps changing

3. Body Clues

☐ Heart fast   ☐ Breathing different   ☐ Muscles tight
☐ Heavy or slow   ☐ Hot   ☐ Cold   ☐ Shaky   ☐ Restless
☐ Stomach feels different   ☐ Head hurts or feels full
☐ Numb or far away   ☐ I cannot find the body clue yet
☐ Another clue: ______________________________

4. What I Want to Do

☐ Move closer   ☐ Move away   ☐ Hide   ☐ Freeze   ☐ Argue
☐ Fix it   ☐ Cry   ☐ Laugh   ☐ Move my body   ☐ Sleep
☐ Get help   ☐ I do not know yet

5. This Might Be About

☐ Something important   ☐ Something unfair   ☐ Something unsafe
☐ Too much input   ☐ A change or surprise   ☐ Missing something or someone
☐ Wanting connection   ☐ Needing space
☐ Hunger, thirst, pain, illness, or tiredness   ☐ I am not ready to know

6. What Might Help

☐ Less sound, light, touch, or talking   ☐ Movement
☐ Food or water   ☐ Rest   ☐ More information   ☐ A clear plan
☐ Someone with me   ☐ Space   ☐ Help solving the problem
☐ Protection or safety help   ☐ Time before deciding

My usable sentence
“Something changed. I notice ____________________. I might need ____________________.”

For the Supporting Adult

Accept pointing, checking, drawing, typing, gesture, or uncertainty. Offer a few choices rather than reading every option aloud. Do not require this page during acute distress. Check physical and safety needs before assuming the experience is emotional.
