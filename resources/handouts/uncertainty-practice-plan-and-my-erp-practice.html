My agreed practice

Success can include feeling uncertain. The goal is to practice the agreed action without the ritual, rather than wait for a calm feeling or a guarantee.

Plan with an OCD-trained clinician

Exposure and response prevention (ERP) pairs approaching an agreed trigger with leaving a compulsion undone. Choose one specific target, a manageable practice, and a clear response-prevention plan together.

Support without solving the doubt

A helper can be present, remind you of your plan, or help you return to a chosen activity. Repeated promises, searching for proof, or reviewing until it feels certain may become another ritual.

Review the practice

Keep real safety and consent. Do not use exposure to override actual danger, medical needs, trauma boundaries, or the person's choice. Comfort, access needs, and sensory supports are not automatically compulsions; clarify their purpose with the clinician.

Show a short example

Trigger: send an ordinary message. Practice: use the agreed normal review, then send it. Ritual left undone: repeated rereading or asking someone to guarantee the wording. Chosen activity: return to lunch while uncertainty is still present.
