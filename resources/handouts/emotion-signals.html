The Goal Is Useful Information, Not a Perfect Label

An emotion name is one kind of data. Body sensations, urges, attention, thoughts, images, energy, and context also matter. Start with whichever signal is easiest to notice.

Clinical Caution

Do not use this sheet to debate someone out of pain, danger, discrimination, sensory overload, or a legitimate boundary. Difficulty naming emotion may reflect interoception, language access, dissociation, overload, development, culture, or simply not knowing yet.
