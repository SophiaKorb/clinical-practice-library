Student Rights & School Accountability

Created and curated by Sophia Korb Cohon, PhD

Start with the concern—not a label

Families and students may encounter missed IEP services, exclusionary discipline, unsafe restraint or seclusion, barriers to evaluation, communication failures, disability discrimination, or possible retaliation after requesting help. The appropriate next step depends on the facts, law, setting, and urgency.

If a child faces immediate danger, contact appropriate emergency or child-protection resources rather than relying on a complaint form.

Choose a pathway

  • Evaluation or eligibility denied: Ask for the decision in writing and for procedural safeguards. Under IDEA, prior written notice applies to certain proposed or refused actions.
  • Services written in the IEP are not happening: Keep a dated record of missed services and responses; request an IEP team review and consult the state education agency's IDEA complaint process.
  • Discipline or school removal: Record dates, duration, reason, and how education continues. IDEA includes specific protections for disciplinary changes of placement.
  • Restraint, seclusion, or safety concern: Request incident reports and governing district/state policies; document injuries and contact appropriate safety authorities where needed.
  • Disability discrimination or retaliation: Review Section 504/Title II civil-rights complaint routes in addition to possible IDEA remedies.

These routes differ; submitting one complaint does not necessarily preserve deadlines for another.

Documentation checklist

  1. Record the incident date, setting, the student's account in their own words, and the observable impact.
  2. Keep copies of IEP/504 plans, evaluations, notices, attendance and disciplinary records, emails, and service logs.
  3. Separate firsthand observations from interpretations and allegations.
  4. Ask for the school's written response, including reasons for decisions.
  5. Keep student-identifying records in secure private storage—not in CPL feedback forms.
