School Support Implementation Log

Created by Sophia Cohon, PhD

Track implementation separately from outcome so the team can tell whether a support failed, was inconsistently provided, or needs adjustment.

Keep notes observable and specific. Record whether the support was available, what happened, and whether access, participation, safety, regulation, or progress changed. This log helps organize follow-up questions. It does not determine legal compliance or eligibility.

Before You Track

Choose one support or one tightly related set of supports. Define what “provided” means before collecting data. If the plan says “breaks as needed,” for example, clarify how the student requests a break, whether adults may prompt it, where the student goes, how long it lasts, and how return occurs.
