School Access, IEP & 504

Created by Sophia Cohon, PhD

Federal reference points for the practical school-access tools in this library

This page anchors the practical school-access tools in this library to current federal sources. The companion guides help parents, clinicians, and support people describe functional barriers, prepare for meetings, and translate clinical concerns into school-access questions. They do not determine legal eligibility and are not legal advice.

Core Idea

A diagnosis can be important evidence, but diagnosis and educational eligibility are not the same question. For an IEP, the team determines whether the child meets IDEA criteria and needs special education because of the disability. For Section 504, the question is whether an impairment substantially limits a major life activity and what aids, services, or adjustments are needed for appropriate access.

The practical tools in this library therefore begin with:

  • What is the student having difficulty accessing, doing, tolerating, communicating, or demonstrating?
  • Under what conditions does the barrier appear?
  • What support directly addresses that barrier?
  • Who will provide it, when, and how will the team know whether it is working?

Trauma & School Functioning

NCTSN Child Trauma Toolkit for Educators

Trauma history or a trauma diagnosis does not automatically determine IEP or Section 504 eligibility. Educational supports should be tied to individualized functional needs, evaluation data, and applicable legal criteria.

Curation Principles

  • Start with function, not labels.
  • Ask what data support the team's conclusion.
  • Distinguish accommodations from specially designed instruction.
  • Do not assume good grades rule out disability-related access problems.
  • Make supports observable: who, what, where, when, how often, and how effectiveness will be evaluated.
  • Include the student's own view of what helps, what is difficult, and what feels usable.
  • Recheck federal and state guidance before relying on legal or procedural details.
