How to use these resources

Begin with the child’s communication and regulation needs and the fit between the environment, expectations and support. The objective is participation and connection, not compliance.

Practice note

Accept gesture, signing, pointing, pictures, AAC, movement and approximate speech as meaningful communication. Match supports to the child and family; do not require eye contact, speech, or emotion labels before recognizing a need. Distinguish age-appropriate sleep support from infant safe-sleep guidance.
