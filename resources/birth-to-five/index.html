Two important age pathways

Birth through age 2: early intervention (IDEA Part C). Age 3 through 5: preschool evaluation and services where eligible (IDEA Part B). Start with the Early intervention & preschool access collection.

Looking for practical clinical material already in the CPL? Open Early Childhood Regulation & Communication or Child, Adolescent, Parenting & Family.

Using this collection

Start with an observable concern and the child's strengths. Choose resources for the actual audience—caregiver, therapist, early intervention team, or preschool staff—and use the child’s age, language, developmental profile, access needs and family priorities to guide adaptation.

Milestone lists support conversations, not diagnosis. Caregiver resources are not substitutes for clinical evaluation, and specialized treatment models require appropriate training. Links lead to the original publishers; check source-specific permission before copying or distributing material.
