Tucson & Pima County: Birth–5 Local Resources

Curated by Sophia Korb Cohon, PhD

Specific referral and family-support options for children from birth through age five in Tucson, Pima County and nearby communities.

Start with age, then residence.

Under 3: AzEIP central referral (888) 592-0140. Ages 3–5: a family’s actual school-district boundary determines the local preschool Child Find office. Tucson mailing addresses are not proof of Tucson Unified district residency. Some local programs restrict services by ZIP code or enrollment criteria.

Developmental concerns & evaluation

Choose by the child’s age and where the family lives.

Referral and use notes

The names, contact details, service areas and entry ages on this page were checked against each organization’s own current information on November 16, 2026. They are not guarantees of enrollment, appointment availability, insurance acceptance, or clinical eligibility. For preschool Child Find, confirm the home district; for AzEIP, start with the state central referral rather than guessing a provider.

Keep the appropriate supports separate. A developmental screening or preschool evaluation, a voluntary home-visiting program, Head Start enrollment, and specialized mental-health treatment serve different purposes. Families may pursue more than one route when indicated.
