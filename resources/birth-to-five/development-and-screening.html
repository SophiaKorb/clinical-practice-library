How to use these resources

Use these sources to notice developmental strengths and questions, choose valid screening and coordinate evaluation. Developmental monitoring alone is not diagnostic testing.

Practice note

A milestone checklist can support a conversation, but it does not replace a validated developmental screener or clinical assessment. If caregivers report regression or a new loss of skills, coordinate prompt medical/developmental evaluation; do not wait for an educational diagnosis.
