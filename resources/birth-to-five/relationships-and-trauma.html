How to use these resources

For infants and preschoolers, consider changes in play, sleep, separation, regulation, relationships and safety without treating every difficulty as evidence of trauma. Match the intervention to the child, caregiver and clinical context.

Practice note

Trauma-informed support and specialized psychotherapy are different. Some caregiver–child treatments are designed to include both together, but the choice and safety of joint sessions require individual assessment. Use trained providers for model-specific treatments.

For other early-childhood topics, return to the Birth–5 resource collections.
