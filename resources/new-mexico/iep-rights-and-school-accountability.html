State complaints and corrective action

New Mexico PED accepts no-cost state complaints alleging violations of special education requirements. Complaints may lead to investigation and corrective action.

Make requests and concerns in writing; preserve dates, evaluations, notices, communications, service logs, and concrete examples. Ask for the current procedural safeguards and identify which agency handles the specific complaint. Some disputes are better addressed through an IEP meeting, state complaint, mediation, due process, or civil rights channels; these options are not interchangeable.

When the school's decision feels unfair

  1. Describe what happened using observable facts and dates, rather than assuming intent.
  2. Request relevant education records and copies of the written decision or prior written notice as applicable.
  3. Document missed IEP services, exclusion from instruction, inappropriate restraint or seclusion, or retaliation concerns separately when relevant.
  4. Review official complaint routes and applicable filing deadlines before submitting a complaint.
  5. Keep student-identifying records private and seek qualified legal or advocacy help when needed.
