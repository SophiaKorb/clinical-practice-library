Choose a starting point

Each path pairs a short explanation with something to try. Families can use these in parent coaching, family therapy, or at home; a clinician should help select and adapt resources when needs are complex.

What keeps happening? Describe the situation without guessing the child's motive (ABC Observation Guide). Why isn't the instruction working? Try a clearer direction, an added support, or a different setup. How do we set limits without losing connection? Distinguish safety, consequences, restitution, and repair. How do we build independence? Use skill-building, meaningful choices, supervision, and gradual release. What should we actually try this week? Make a small, observable experiment and review it together.

A simple learning sequence

  1. Observe: Describe exactly what happened and when it was easier.
  2. Understand: Consider unmet skills, fatigue, sensory needs, stress, communication, relationship patterns, and actual choice.
  3. Decide: Pick one adult action, one useful support, and one skill to teach or practice.
  4. Try: Rehearse while calm; respond consistently but adapt when the plan is not working.
  5. Review: Ask what improved, what still felt hard, and what the child noticed. Adjust instead of escalating punishments.

Important distinctions: Understanding a behavior is not excusing harm. A limit is not the same as a punishment. Support is not something a child must earn. When safety is at risk, protective action and a tailored safety plan take priority.

Use with extra care

Do not rely on a general parenting handout alone for credible threats, abuse, violence, runaway risk, self-harm, severe distress, coercive control, or significant medical or developmental concerns. Address immediate danger, protect the people involved, and obtain individualized help. For trauma-specific materials, see Caregiver, Family and Youth Trauma Resources.

For clinicians: Select a handout, adapt examples for developmental level and communication access, practice the intervention in session, and follow up on what occurred. These materials do not supersede the separate Parenting Guide Edition 7.7 or its approval process.
