Understanding the labels
★ ESA = The provider publicly advertises acceptance of Arizona ESA or ClassWallet payments. This is a provider
statement, not ADE approval of every charge. ◇ Disability-focused = The program specifically serves or adapts
services for people with disabilities. FREE / LOW COST = Program description; confirm current pricing and eligibility.
County boundaries matter: Oracle, San Manuel, Mammoth, Kearny and Winkelman are in Pinal County; Tucson, Oro
Valley, Catalina and Marana are in Pima County. Some resources have geographic or insurance restrictions. San Manuel
Community Center is scheduled to close for renovation in late November 2026; the adjacent park remains open.

Understanding ESA funding
ESA funds are restricted education funds. Provider acceptance is separate from whether a particular class, therapy,
registration charge, equipment item or other cost qualifies under current ADE rules.
Ask whether payment is by ClassWallet Pay Vendor/Direct Pay, ESA debit card or reimbursement; collect a detailed

An ESA marker is NOT insurance acceptance or a guarantee of reimbursement. Confirm both service eligibility and the

    For friendship: choose a recurring activity with the same children rather than a string of unrelated one-time outings.
