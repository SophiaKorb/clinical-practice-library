Southern Arizona · Transition-age and family support

I AM YOU 360

A Tucson nonprofit offering personalized hygiene assistance, a small-home housing program, and opportunities for community connection, self-development, and practical life skills.

Before referring: Not every program has the same eligibility. Inquire directly about a young person’s age, foster-care or housing circumstances, LGBTQ+ inclusion, pregnancy/parenting circumstances, available spaces, accessibility, cost, consent, and how to apply. Being described here does not establish enrollment or eligibility.

Hygiene Empowerment Program

Personalized full-size hygiene products for underserved youth and families. The organization describes replenishment every six weeks; confirm registration, current supply and pickup arrangements.

Provider program and contact information

Small Home Experience

Tucson small-home housing program with financial planning and practical supports for qualifying young adults. Historical county program information describes an 18–22 target group of youth aging out of foster care and/or experiencing homelessness, but current admissions, availability, costs and requirements need direct confirmation.

Provider housing program information · County resource entry (historical)

Community building

Group connection, mentorship and self-worth activities, including opportunities for participants to build relationships and practical life skills. Specific group schedules, participant ages and access procedures should be confirmed directly.

City of Tucson program account, July 24, 2026

Practical and wraparound-style support

The organization and city materials describe a whole-person approach, including housing-related support, life-skills learning and community connections. Ask which specific individualized or coordinated services are currently offered; this entry does not establish that a licensed clinical wraparound program is available.

Official organization website

Contact and eligibility questions

Official site currently lists the office and hygiene pickup location as 1131 S. Van Buren Ave, Tucson, AZ 85711; the separate Small Home Experience is described at 1101 S. Van Buren Ave. Do not confuse the office with the housing site.

Phone: 520-372-2714. Email: iamyou360@hotmail.com. Provider lists hygiene drop-off/pickup office hours Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 9 AM–noon; verify before visiting.

Ask which services are available to current or former foster youth, LGBTQ+ youth, pregnant or parenting youth, unaccompanied youth, and youth experiencing homelessness. These are groups to ask about, not a verified eligibility list for each program.
