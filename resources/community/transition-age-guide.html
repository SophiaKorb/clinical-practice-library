Local community supplement · November 2026

LGBTQ+ community resources in Southern Arizona

Eight local directory listings added to this online guide for easier access; they are not part of the original October 25, 2026 numbered guide or approved PDF. Confirm current program ages, enrollment, event access, legal rules, privacy and availability with each provider. Respect the person's choice about disclosure; never assume youth want family or school involvement.

  • Eon Youth Lounge

    SAAF / Thornhill Lopez Center on 4th · Ages 13–24 · 526 N. 4th Avenue, Tucson

    LGBTQ+ and allied peer connection, identity support, school GSA outreach, art and game programming, food, clothing, toiletries and laundry. Check the current youth programming calendar, hours and access details with SAAF.

    See CPL directory notes

  • Pride Student Space

    University of Arizona Campus Community Connections · Student Union Memorial Center, Room 404AA, Tucson

    LGBTQ+2s community space and campus programming; university describes its community as including students, staff, alumni, families and guests. Public-event access varies by event; check organizer first.

    See CPL directory notes

  • Fluxx Productions: arts, events and mutual aid

    Fluxx Productions / SAGA · Tucson · event-specific locations

    Queer arts, social events, activism and community health/mutual aid programs; confirm each event's age, location and admission details.

    See CPL directory notes

  • STARS — Sonoran Transgender Advocacy, Resources & Support

    Together, We Shine Brighter · Tucson / Sonoran Desert region

    Transgender and gender-nonconforming support, advocacy, events, moderated Discord, guidance and community connection.

    See CPL directory notes

  • STARS name and gender-marker change guide

    STARS · Arizona / Pima County-specific procedures

    Guidance and forms for court name changes and IDs. Because administrative rules and litigation change, independently check current court and government requirements before acting.

    See CPL directory notes

  • STARS updating records after a name change

    STARS · Across Arizona and federal records

    Detailed agency-by-agency follow-up checklist for employment, insurance, banks, travel, education, and other records.

    See CPL directory notes

  • Name & gender marker change clinic

    Fluxx Productions / SAGA · Pima County · clinics twice yearly

    Volunteer/student-lawyer-assisted paperwork clinics; between-clinic support and current downloadable guides. Confirm the next event and current legal requirements.

    See CPL directory notes

  • Community Health Project and mutual aid

    Fluxx Productions · Tucson · program-specific

    Organizer describes wellness and mutual-aid services including clothing swaps, housing-related assistance and sexual-health education; confirm eligibility and current availability.

    See CPL directory notes
