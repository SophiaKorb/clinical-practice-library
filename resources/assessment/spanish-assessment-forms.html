SPANISH ASSESSMENT FORMS – OFFICIAL VERSIONS & CAVEATS

Use official translations. Do not translate standardized items locally.

CORE-10

Official Spanish versions are available through CORE System Trust.

Access: CORE Spanish downloads

Rights: CORE measures are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 for unchanged noncommercial reproduction with attribution.

YP-CORE

For Latin American Spanish-speaking adolescents, use the official Latin America version rather than the Spain version.

Direct form: YP-CORE Latin America unisex

Reason: CORE's research found that the Spain translation was not suitable as the default YP-CORE for Latin American adolescents.

PSC / PSC-17

Mass General provides official Spanish PSC materials.

Access: Mass General PSC resources

Storage rule for this library: link to the official source rather than copying the copyrighted file unless redistribution permission is confirmed.

SWYC

TEAM UP provides Spanish components, complete age-specific sets and bilingual Spanish-English forms.

Access: SWYC Spanish translations

Caveat: TEAM UP states that translations are not automatically equivalent to the English version and recommends attention to the validation evidence for each translation.

CATS

CATS is available in Spanish through the developers.

Access and contact: CATS official page

Caveat: use as a trauma/PTSD screen, not a stand-alone diagnosis.

PCL-5 AND PC-PTSD-5

VA has staff-confirmed Spanish translations available by request.

Access: VA Spanish PTSD measure translations

Caveat: VA states these Spanish translations have not been validated in published research, so this limitation should be acknowledged when used.

COLUMBIA / C-SSRS

U.S. Spanish versions are available through the Columbia toolkit/translation system.

Access: Columbia translations

The Columbia Lighthouse Project states that healthcare/community use and training are free.

CRAFFT 2.1 / 2.1+N

The official CRAFFT site provides Spanish (LatAm) and Spanish (Spain) versions.

Access: CRAFFT official forms

For this Arizona-facing library, Spanish (LatAm) is the default unless the patient's language background suggests otherwise.

AUDIT

WHO makes the AUDIT manual available in Spanish as well as English.

Access: WHO AUDIT

GENERAL RULES

Use the official translated instrument version when one exists.

Do not translate items, response anchors, scoring language or cutoffs yourself and then treat the result as standardized.

Document language and version used when clinically relevant.

For bilingual patients, language preference, literacy, acculturation and regional vocabulary may affect interpretation even when an official translation exists.

A translated screener is still a screener.

M-CHAT-R/F

Official M-CHAT-R/F translations include multiple Spanish versions, including Western Hemisphere options.

Access: https://www.mchatscreen.com/mchat-rf/translations/

Rights: copyrighted instrument; use official posted versions unchanged and follow the developer's permissions.

Caveat: choose the regional version that best fits the family rather than assuming all Spanish forms are interchangeable.
