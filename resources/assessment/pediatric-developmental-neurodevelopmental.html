PEDIATRIC, DEVELOPMENTAL & NEURODEVELOPMENTAL ASSESSMENT

Clinician Start-Here Guide

USE THIS SHELF FOR

Broad pediatric screening, early-childhood development/behavior, ADHD assessment, autism diagnostic clarification, executive-function concerns, and developmental differential diagnosis.

BROAD PEDIATRIC FRONT END

PSC-17

Best use: low-burden broad pediatric psychosocial screening when the referral question is initially diffuse.

Respondents: parent/caregiver and youth self-report versions are available.

Scoring: total score 15 or higher is Mass General's recommended high-risk threshold. Attention 7 or higher, Internalizing 5 or higher, and Externalizing 7 or higher are recommended high-risk subscale thresholds.

Interpretation: elevated scores identify broad psychosocial impairment or a domain needing focused assessment.

Do not interpret as: a psychiatric diagnosis, BASC replacement, or proof of a specific disorder.

Spanish: official PSC and PSC-17 Spanish forms are available from Mass General.

Official source: Mass General Pediatric Symptom Checklist

UNDER AGE 5

SWYC

Best use: broad developmental, behavioral/emotional, autism-risk and family-context screening from infancy through preschool.

Score components separately using the current age-specific rules. There is no single SWYC total score.

Do not interpret as: formal developmental, language, cognitive, adaptive, or autism diagnosis.

Rights rule: official forms are free to access; do not modify. Electronic or nonstandard implementation requires attention to TEAM UP use conditions.

Official source: TEAM UP SWYC

ADHD

Use developmental history, impairment and cross-setting collateral as the core assessment.

CDC/AAP guidance emphasizes reports from parents or guardians, school staff and other involved adults, rating scales and other sources, and evaluation of alternative or co-occurring conditions.

A single rating scale is not the diagnosis.

Parent-teacher discrepancy should trigger inquiry into demands, structure, relationship, masking, anxiety, sleep, learning, sensory load, medication timing and opportunity to observe.

Official source: CDC Clinical Care of ADHD

EXECUTIVE FUNCTION

CHEXI / TEXI / ADEXI

Best use: structured descriptive collateral about everyday executive-function difficulty when a free rating is useful.

Do not interpret as: BRIEF equivalence, ADHD diagnosis, or formal neuropsychological EF measurement.

Use developmental course, impairment and differential assessment alongside scores.

Source: CHEXI developer site

AUTISM

Screening and self-report measures can support formulation or identify need for specialty evaluation.

A comprehensive autism diagnosis should integrate developmental and social-communication history, restricted/repetitive/sensory pattern, function, differential diagnosis, collateral and appropriately trained direct or structured assessment when indicated.

Do not convert AQ, CAT-Q, M-CHAT or another screener into a diagnosis.

Formal ADOS-2 or other proprietary diagnostic tools remain training and access dependent.

ADHD VS AUTISM VS TRAUMA

Ask first about developmental onset and function.

ADHD is supported when attention and impulse regulation problems are persistent and cross-situational.

Autism is supported when developmental social-communication differences and restricted/repetitive/sensory patterns are longstanding.

Trauma is supported when symptoms have a meaningful temporal or functional relationship to threat exposure, while recognizing that pre-existing vulnerability and comorbidity are common.

Topography alone does not identify function.

FORMAL TESTING BOUNDARIES

No free substitute should be presented as equivalent to normed broadband behavioral profiles, formal cognitive testing, normed adaptive behavior, formal academic achievement testing, normed language testing, or formal visual-motor/perceptual testing.

STOP RULE

Stop when DSM symptoms, impairment, developmental course, cross-setting evidence and major alternatives are adequately addressed for the clinical decision. Escalate when the remaining uncertainty is high-stakes or formal services require specialty or normed evaluation.
