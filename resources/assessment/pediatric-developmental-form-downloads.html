SWYC — ENGLISH AND SPANISH

Status: YELLOW, official-source link.

Official translations clearinghouse: SWYC Translations

The TEAM UP Center provides Spanish components, age-specific forms, and bilingual Spanish-English forms.

Important limitation: translated SWYC versions are not automatically psychometrically equivalent to the English version. TEAM UP explicitly recommends attention to the evidence supporting each translation.

AUTISM TODDLER SCREEN — M-CHAT-R/F

Status: YELLOW, link to official source.

Official source and permissions: https://www.mchatscreen.com/mchat-rf/

Use: two-stage parent-report autism-likelihood screening for toddlers within the instrument's stated age/use conditions.

Rights: free for clinical, research and educational use under the authors' terms, but copyrighted and not open source. Reposting or electronic distribution can require a license.

Official translations, including multiple Spanish versions: https://www.mchatscreen.com/mchat-rf/translations/

Boundary: positive screening requires developmental/autism follow-up and does not establish diagnosis.
