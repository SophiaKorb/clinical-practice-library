FORM DOWNLOADS – FUNCTIONING & FORMAL TESTING

Official access notes

WHODAS 2.0

Status: YELLOW.

Official source: WHO WHODAS 2.0

Use: generic adult disability/functioning.

Rights caution: WHO's current page directs users to licensing for reproduction, electronic-record embedding and other reproduction. This library therefore links to the official source rather than storing or recreating a copy.

Boundary: WHODAS is not a substitute for normed adaptive behavior.

ADAPTIVE BEHAVIOR

Status: PROPRIETARY / LICENSED when normed scores are required.

Examples: Vineland or ABAS as appropriate to referral question and organizational access.

Do not create open substitutes for formal adaptive composites.

COGNITIVE ABILITY

Status: FORMAL LICENSED TESTING REQUIRED when normed IQ/index scores are the decision variable.

Do not build a homebrew IQ battery.

ACADEMIC ACHIEVEMENT

Status: FORMAL LICENSED TESTING REQUIRED when normed reading/writing/math achievement is needed.

Informal probes and records may support formulation but not replace standardized achievement scores.

LANGUAGE / COMMUNICATION

Status: SPECIALTY/NORMED TESTING when formal receptive, expressive or pragmatic-language scores are needed.

Refer to or coordinate with SLP as appropriate.

VISUAL-MOTOR / VISUAL-PERCEPTUAL

Status: SPECIALTY/NORMED TESTING when formal scores are needed.

Informal observation should be labeled as informal observation.

REPORTING RULE

Always name the level of evidence: screen, interview, collateral, observation, informal probe, or formal standardized test.
