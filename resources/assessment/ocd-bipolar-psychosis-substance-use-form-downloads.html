FORM DOWNLOADS – OCD, BIPOLAR, PSYCHOSIS & SUBSTANCE USE

Official forms and access notes

OCD — FOCI / C-FOCI

Status: YELLOW, official APA forms.

Official source: APA DSM-5-TR Assessment Measures

Use official Level 2 forms and preserve wording/version. Electronic or other use may have additional rights conditions.

Boundary: quick severity support, not a stand-alone OCD diagnosis or substitute for a fuller clinician-rated scale when that depth is required.

BIPOLAR / MANIA — ASRM

Status: YELLOW, official APA form.

Official source: APA DSM-5-TR Assessment Measures

Use the current form and scoring instructions.

Boundary: symptom severity screening does not establish episodicity or bipolar disorder.

PSYCHOSIS RISK — PQ-B

Status: GREEN for clinical use from developer source.

Official source: UCSF Loewy Lab PQ

The developer states that PQ versions including PQ-B are free for research, clinical purposes and EHR platforms when users are not charged per use.

Boundary: specialty risk screener, not a general psychosis test.

ALCOHOL — AUDIT

Status: OFFICIAL WHO RESOURCE.

Official manual: WHO AUDIT

WHO provides the AUDIT manual in English and Spanish.

Boundary: screening/brief assessment does not replace withdrawal-risk or full substance-use evaluation.

ADOLESCENT SUBSTANCE USE — CRAFFT 2.1 / 2.1+N

Status: GREEN from developer source.

Official forms page: CRAFFT Get the CRAFFT

The site provides clinician-administered and self-administered versions and both Spanish (LatAm) and Spanish (Spain) translations.

For Arizona, prefer Spanish (LatAm) unless there is a reason to use the Spain version.

Boundary: screening supports brief counseling and follow-up; it does not create a substance-use diagnosis on its own.

DRUG USE — DAST-10

Status: GREEN for noncommercial clinical/research/training use with attribution.

NIH Common Data Elements source: https://cde.nlm.nih.gov/formView?tinyId=QJPG1TWwl

NIDA Data Share information: https://datashare.nida.nih.gov/instrument/drug-abuse-screening-test

Rights: the DAST-10 is copyrighted by Harvey A. Skinner/CAMH but may be reproduced for noncommercial clinical, research and training purposes with credit.

Boundary: screens drug-related problems other than alcohol/tobacco; it does not replace substance-specific history, withdrawal/overdose assessment or diagnosis.
