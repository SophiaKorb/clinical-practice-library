FORM DOWNLOADS – TRAUMA & SAFETY

Official forms and access notes

PCL-5 — ENGLISH

Status: GREEN.

Rights: VA National Center for PTSD states that the PCL-5 is public domain and not copyrighted.

Direct official form: PCL-5 Past Month fillable PDF

Use: screening, provisional assessment, symptom severity and monitoring.

Boundary: structured clinical interview remains the diagnostic gold standard.

PC-PTSD-5 — ENGLISH

Status: GREEN.

Rights: VA states the PC-PTSD-5 is public domain and not copyrighted.

Direct official form: PC-PTSD-5 PDF

Boundary: positive screens require further assessment.

PCL-5 / PC-PTSD-5 — SPANISH

Status: OFFICIAL VERSION AVAILABLE BY REQUEST.

VA states that confirmed Spanish translations are available by request and have not been validated in published research.

Request/source page: VA Spanish PTSD Measure Translations

Do not substitute a locally translated form.

CATS — CHILD & ADOLESCENT TRAUMA SCREEN

Status: GREEN TO ACCESS; keep official-source versions unchanged.

ISTSS states that CATS is freely accessible and has no copyright or licensing fees.

Official forms and guide: CATS official page

Spanish: available through the developers; contact information is provided on the official page.

Boundary: positive CATS results require clinical follow-up and should not be used alone to diagnose PTSD.

COLUMBIA PROTOCOL / C-SSRS

Status: GREEN for healthcare/community use.

The Columbia Lighthouse Project states that healthcare/community use and training are free and that special permission is not required to use the tools in a setting or embed them in an EHR.

Official healthcare toolkit: Columbia Healthcare Toolkit

Official translations: Columbia Translations

Spanish: U.S. Spanish versions are available through the toolkit/translation system.
