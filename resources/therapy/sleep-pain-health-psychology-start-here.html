Clinical Use

  • For insomnia, assess sleep opportunity, schedule, circadian pattern, apnea risk, parasomnias, restless legs/movement, substances, medications, and medical contributors.
  • CBT-I is more than sleep hygiene. Use a coherent protocol and monitor sleep data.
  • In chronic pain, track functioning, pacing, values, activity, sleep, mood, coping, and participation rather than pain reduction alone.
  • Validate pain as real while addressing fear, avoidance, deconditioning, and unhelpful coping patterns when relevant.
  • Coordinate with medical care when red flags, medication issues, or changes in physical status require evaluation.
