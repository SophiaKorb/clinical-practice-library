Clinical Boundaries

  • Trauma-informed care is not the same thing as evidence-based PTSD treatment.
  • Stabilization can be clinically necessary, but indefinite skills-only treatment should not automatically substitute for effective trauma-focused care when PTSD is the target and the patient is ready.
  • Assess dissociation, psychosis, substance use, acute safety, and medical factors when they materially affect treatment planning.
  • Choose the protocol based on the patient's presentation, preferences, evidence, and clinician competence, not therapist comfort alone.

Rights / Training

Link to official VA, TF-CBT, and NCTSN sources. Some individual resources permit broader use; others have model-specific or source-specific terms. Do not repackage treatment manuals whose redistribution terms are unclear.
