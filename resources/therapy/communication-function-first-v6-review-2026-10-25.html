5. Supported Assent

About this activity: you can say yes, no, not sure, break, stop, or tell me more.

YESI want to do this.
NOI do not want this.
NOT SUREI have not decided.
BREAKPause for now.
STOPStop this activity.
TELL ME MOREI want information.

OTHER MESSAGE: _____________________________________

You can change your answer. You can use your usual communication method.
For the clinician: Check how the person communicates yes, no, and uncertainty. Silence or lack of objection is not assent. Respect verbal and nonverbal dissent. An answer alone does not establish informed consent or decisional capacity.
