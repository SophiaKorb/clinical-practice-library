Why this is 2D without arbitrary axes

A conventional scatterplot compresses 26 features to two coordinates. The apparent distance between dots can suggest a real diagnostic geography. This atlas instead displays the underlying ratings directly: rows are examples, columns are features, and comparisons are visible rather than hidden inside a projection.

What to ask before diagnosis

  • When did these experiences begin, and what is their course?
  • What actually interferes with life, and in what settings?
  • How do development, culture, trauma, medical conditions, and substance exposures change the interpretation?
  • Which distinguishing criteria and alternative explanations must be checked?
