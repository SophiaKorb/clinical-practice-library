Open & Shareable Therapy Resources

Curated by Sophia Cohon, PhD

CBT, ACT, DBT-informed skills, trauma, substance use, and scalable psychological interventions.

This guide is for material clinicians can actually use, teach from, print when permitted, or direct patients and trainees toward. It distinguishes material that can be redistributed from resources that are excellent but should be linked rather than copied.

A resource being free to download does not automatically make it free to redistribute.

A. Original Material in This Library

Original clinical teaching and patient/family materials include CBT-informed, emotion-regulation, cognitive-flexibility, parenting, transition, sensory, school-access, and DBT-informed resources.

DBT, Plain and Useful includes a Four-Module Skills Curriculum, Clinician Guide, Patient Handout Pack, and Teaching & Practice Guide. These are DBT-informed educational materials, not official Linehan DBT materials and not a substitute for formal DBT training.

Linehan DBT skills handouts and worksheets are copyrighted/licensed material. Keep legally obtained copies in private professional holdings as permitted rather than in the external shareable library.

Distribution Rule

Green: copy/share when the source clearly permits it.

Yellow: link to the official source unless the specific item grants broader permission.

Red: do not put copies in the external library.

The standard is not “Can I find a PDF?” The standard is “Would I be comfortable explaining to the author or publisher why this copy is in a public-facing professional library?”
