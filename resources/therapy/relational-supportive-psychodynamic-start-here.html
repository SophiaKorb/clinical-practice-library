Current Status of This Shelf

This is intentionally an orientation and formulation shelf, not a pretend free psychodynamic manual. The private professional library contains licensed/reference material, but there is not yet a contemporary, comprehensive, clearly redistributable psychodynamic treatment manual that belongs in the external library.

Use This Shelf For

Therapeutic relationship, supportive psychotherapy, relational formulation, recurring interpersonal patterns, defenses, affect tolerance, meaning, developmental context, and treatment-frame thinking.

Useful Open Orientation Resource

Present-Centered Therapy for PTSD

This is a PTSD treatment rather than a general supportive-psychotherapy manual, but its structure is useful as an example of a present-focused, problem-oriented, validating treatment with active listening, reflection, emotional expression, and problem solving.

Practice Frame

  • Clarify what kind of therapy you are doing. “Supportive” should not mean aimless conversation.
  • Track the patient's goals, current functioning, recurrent relationship patterns, and the therapeutic relationship itself when relevant.
  • Match interpretation depth to ego strength, affect tolerance, reflective capacity, and the treatment frame.
  • Use clarification before confrontation and confrontation before deep interpretation when more direct approaches are clinically appropriate.
  • Avoid turning every interaction into transference analysis.
  • Support reality testing, agency, adaptive defenses, relationships, work, and daily functioning.
  • Attend to countertransference as clinical information, not as proof about the patient.
  • Maintain boundaries that are predictable, explainable, and proportionate to the setting.

Private / Licensed Material

PDM-2 and commercial psychodynamic/relational books remain formulation/reference material and are not copied into the external shareable collection.

Gap to Fill Later

A current, practical, legally shareable supportive/relational clinician manual would strengthen this shelf. Until one is identified, an honest gap is preferable to a home-made imitation of a proprietary treatment text.
