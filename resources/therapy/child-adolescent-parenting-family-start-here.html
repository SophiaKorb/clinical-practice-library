Clinical Use

  • Define whether the treatment target sits primarily in the child, caregiver-child interaction, family system, school/environment, or a combination.
  • Parent work is not simply “teach the parent to be stricter.” Match intervention to function, developmental level, caregiver capacity, trauma, communication, and neurodevelopmental needs.
  • Direct child psychotherapy, parent management training, live coaching, family therapy, and school intervention solve different problems.
  • Include the child or adolescent's perspective and assent when developmentally appropriate.
  • For young children, consider whether changing caregiver behavior and the environment will produce more benefit than asking the child for abstract insight.
