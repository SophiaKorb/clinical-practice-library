Scope, sources and clinical use
This is an original menu of example psychotherapy planning language, not an AthenaOne template or a complete DSM catalog. It covers major outpatient diagnosis families and common overlapping presentations. Selection does not establish a diagnosis. Use developmental, cultural and medical context, client preference, and clinician competence to choose treatment.
Example goals, criteria and measurement wording are authored for this library; they are not quotations or prescribed thresholds from the linked guidelines. Source links support the treatment direction, with limitations stated for each presentation. NICE guidance is from the UK and is a clinical reference, not Arizona billing policy. The ISSTD adult guideline is specialist consensus guidance from 2011. Diagnosis-specific assessment instruments require appropriate training and permission; no protected items or scoring rules are reproduced.
Adult sources do not automatically apply to children. Adapt with an age-specific protocol, caregiver involvement when appropriate, and supported assent. Co-occurring diagnoses may change sequencing. Acute risk, medical instability, mania, or new psychosis requires assessment and an appropriate care setting before routine outpatient target work.
Use this public tool with generic wording. Entries stay in this tab’s memory, are not saved, and disappear on reload. Copy or print only to an approved destination. Document only interventions actually planned or provided. Review dates and signature requirements follow your practice and payer policies.
