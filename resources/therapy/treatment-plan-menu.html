Sources, clinical scope, and previous plan builder

These are original, editable ideas, not a licensed treatment planner, diagnosis algorithm, or required sequence. Treatment appropriateness depends on formulation, cultural and individual context, access, consent, and clinician qualifications. Exposure practices and eating-disorder treatment require appropriately qualified guidance; medical concerns require suitable medical assessment and coordination.

Standardized measures are identified separately with external source links. An individually devised frequency count or rating is not a validated symptom measure. The prior full plan builder is preserved outside this menu.
