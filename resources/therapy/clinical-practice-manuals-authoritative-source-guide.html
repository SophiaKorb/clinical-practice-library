Clinical Practice Manuals

Curated by Sophia Cohon, PhD

A small, high-value set of clinical manuals, training hubs, and official practice resources.

The emphasis is on materials that are useful in real clinical work and can be accessed through authoritative sources. This guide intentionally links to official sources rather than redistributing commercial, licensed, or source-uncertain PDFs.

Curation Rules

  • Prefer primary/official sources over copies of uncertain provenance.
  • Keep commercial and licensed manuals private.
  • Distinguish a useful historical resource from a current treatment anchor.
  • Link to frequently updated federal and institutional guidance rather than freezing an old copy.
  • Do not treat availability on the internet as permission to redistribute.
  • Recheck version, training requirements, and scope before clinical implementation.
