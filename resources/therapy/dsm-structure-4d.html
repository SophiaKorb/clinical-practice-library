How the positions are calculated

Each profile has 22 ordinal feature ratings (0–3). Ratings are converted to unit-length vectors, mean-centered, and projected onto the first three principal components calculated by a deterministic power-iteration eigensolver. 3D point positions are not manually assigned. Similarity comparisons use cosine similarity in the full 22-dimensional feature space, not 3D distance. Overlap is descriptive, not a differential diagnosis.

Evidence and limits: The cross-cutting approach is informed conceptually by HiTOP and the NIMH RDoC framework, neither of which endorses these illustrative weights or geometry.
