5. Supported Assent

For this activity, which answer fits you right now?

YESI want to continue.
NOI do not want this.
NOT SUREI need more information.
BREAKPause for now.
STOPStop this activity.
You can change your answer. You can use your usual communication method.
For the clinician: A response is not by itself proof of understanding, decision-making capacity, or legal consent. Notice and respect dissent, including nonverbal dissent.
