Formulate Before Choosing a Worksheet

Identify the loop that is keeping the problem going: withdrawal and loss of reinforcement, threat prediction and avoidance, self-criticism and disengagement, rumination, reassurance, sleep disruption, skill deficit, or another pattern. Then choose the intervention because it changes that loop, not because it is a familiar CBT technique.

Behavioral Activation: Make the Experiment Small Enough to Happen

When depression has narrowed life, do not begin with an ideal routine. Identify one behavior that could increase contact with mastery, pleasure, connection, movement, responsibility, or meaning. Specify when and where it will happen, anticipate the point of friction, and review what happened without turning noncompletion into a character judgment.

Rights / Training

WHO PM+, Step-by-Step, and several WHO psychological-intervention resources carry explicit Creative Commons terms on their publication pages. Retain attribution and license terms. VA resources should be linked from the official source; check each document for third-party material before redistribution. Do not treat a brief guide as proof of competency in specialized CBT protocols.

Gaps to Keep Visible

A single contemporary general CBT manual cannot answer every disorder-specific treatment question. Use the dedicated Anxiety/ERP, Trauma/PTSD, Sleep/Pain, and SUD shelves when the treatment target becomes specific.
