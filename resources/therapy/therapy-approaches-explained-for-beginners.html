Beginner field guide

What are these different therapy approaches?

A beginner-friendly tour for interns and case managers.

CBT

Explores the interaction between thoughts, emotions and actions, and tests new responses.

Example: A person expecting rejection experiments with one small social interaction.

ACT

Builds psychological flexibility: moving toward values even when difficult thoughts and feelings are present.

DBT

Combines acceptance and change with skills for emotion regulation, distress tolerance and relationships.

Exposure and ERP

Helps people approach feared situations while reducing unnecessary avoidance and rituals.

Educational guide, not a treatment protocol. Sophia Korb Cohon, PhD.
