Start with what you need

Build a treatment planChoose a diagnosis or presentation, then adapt needs, goals, objectives, interventions and progress measures. I need a treatment directionGo to the relevant clinical shelf and start with its “Start Here” guide. I need something usable in sessionOpen the practical toolkit for the relevant domain. I need something to shareMove to patient/family education or the relevant handout pack. I need to clarify what is going onUse Assessment & Screening when the decision depends on measurement, diagnosis, risk, or differential assessment.

Clinical route

1 · FORMULATEWhat is the target?
Define the problem in functional, observable terms.
2 · ANCHORChoose one treatment frame
Avoid mixing techniques before deciding what model is doing the work.
3 · ADAPTMake the treatment accessible
Adjust language, sensory load, pacing, response mode, and executive demands as needed.
4 · CHECKMeasure what changed
Use outcomes, function, risk, and patient experience to decide whether to continue or reformulate.

Use the shelves well

  1. Start with the domain guide rather than opening ten resources at once.
  2. Choose one treatment anchor, then add techniques that fit the formulation.
  3. Use patient/family materials when they improve shared understanding or follow-through.
  4. Use the Español shelf for Spanish patient/family versions with clear validation metadata.
  5. Check training and distribution notes before copying, adapting, or teaching from an outside protocol.
  6. Use assessment resources when treatment depends on diagnostic clarification, measurement, risk, or formal testing.

Curation standard

Prefer current primary or official sources. Distinguish treatment protocols from orientation pages, self-help from therapist-delivered treatment, and free access from permission to redistribute.

A PDF existing on the internet is not a licensing strategy. Public access and permission to redistribute are different questions.
