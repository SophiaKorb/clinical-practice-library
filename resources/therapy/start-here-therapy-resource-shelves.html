Therapy Resource Shelves

Curated by Sophia Cohon, PhD

Use this section when you need a treatment anchor, clinician tool, or patient/family handout.

The shelves are organized by topic and treatment target, not by author. Original library materials and authoritative outside resources live together in the relevant topic shelf.

How to Use the Shelves

  1. Start with the domain guide in the relevant folder.
  2. Choose one treatment anchor rather than mixing techniques without a formulation.
  3. Open patient and family handouts when you need practical material for patients or caregivers.
  4. Use the Español shelf for Spanish patient/family versions.
  5. Check training and distribution notes before copying, adapting, or teaching from an outside protocol.
  6. Use Assessment & Screening when the treatment decision depends on measurement, diagnosis, risk, or differential assessment.

Rights Key

Green: source clearly permits noncommercial copying/redistribution under stated terms, or the material is original to this library.

Yellow: link to the official source. Do not repackage unless the specific item grants permission.

Red: keep private or licensed. Do not place copies in the external library.

Curation Standard

Prefer current primary or official sources. Distinguish treatment protocols from orientation pages, self-help from therapist-delivered treatment, and free access from permission to redistribute.

A PDF existing on the internet is not a licensing strategy.
