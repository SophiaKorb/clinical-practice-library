What changed besides treatment?
Worked case: improvement with persistent support needs

A fictional adult begins attending three of four appointments with a visible calendar and a support person's departure cue, compared with one of four previously. Distress is lower and the person finds the reminder helpful. Attendance improved under supported conditions; unaided prospective memory and need for the cue remain unchanged.

Document both the benefit and the continuing support need. Do not remove an effective accommodation just to claim independence. Track whether the support remains available, whether missed visits reflect transportation, and whether the person can manage another meaningful routine.

Interpretation layer: what would make us change course?
  • Symptoms improve but participation remains limited: reassess access, skills, environment, and support needs.
  • Completion improves while distress, fatigue, prompting, or coercion rises: revise the plan; apparent productivity may conceal burden.
  • Progress appears only in therapy: examine generalization and whether the everyday environment contains the needed supports.
  • No change: check intervention fit, opportunity, fidelity, measurement, competing explanations, and the person's goals.
  • Sudden functional decline or safety change: follow an appropriate medical or crisis assessment route.

Write the review in a usable way

“During [period], [task/outcome] changed from [baseline] to [review] in [context], using [supports]. [Distress/burden/agency] changed by [observation]. [Remaining limitation] persists. The client reports [perspective]. Continue/change [action], with review on [date].”

For standardized measures, use authorized versions, appropriate language/norms, and recommended interpretation. Do not infer reliable change from an arbitrary raw-score difference. Administrative disability conclusions require their own applicable criteria and evaluation.

APA assessment guidance anchors careful interpretation. MSKTC rehabilitation materials provide functional education. This matrix is original educational synthesis and has no validated cutoff.
