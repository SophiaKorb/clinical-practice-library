Colorado's IEP framework

Colorado implements IDEA alongside its Exceptional Children's Educational Act (ECEA). IEPs document individualized educational services and supports. Families can find current IEP procedural guidance and translated procedural safeguards through the Colorado Department of Education.

Steps for families

  1. Describe the student's strengths and barriers with examples from school and home.
  2. Contact the district about an evaluation request and obtain the current procedural safeguards.
  3. Bring evaluations, classroom work, records, and questions to the IEP meeting.
  4. Ask how goals, accommodations, related services, and progress reports will be implemented.
  5. Keep the written IEP, notices, and a dated follow-up record.

When transferring schools or states

Keep copies of the most recent IEP, eligibility determination, evaluations, service schedules, and recent progress reports. Contact the receiving district's special education office promptly. IDEA has requirements for comparable services when a student transfers with an IEP; the receiving team may need to review or develop a new plan.
