About

About the library

This is the web edition of the selected external clinical library. Original materials, official resources, open-access tools, and curated guides are organized together by topic. Original materials identify Sophia Cohon, PhD as creator; outside resources retain their original authorship and source.

Original materials may be shared for clinical care, teaching, supervision, training, and other noncommercial professional or educational use. Commercial, licensed, source-uncertain, or restricted materials are not republished here. Where appropriate, the site links to a legitimate primary source instead.

These resources support clinical thinking and patient/family education. They do not replace individualized assessment, emergency evaluation, treatment-model training, supervision, or applicable law and policy.
