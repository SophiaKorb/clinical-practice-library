Clinical Note

Placement itself, disrupted attachment, uncertainty, contact with birth family, loyalty conflicts, repeated transitions, and system involvement can all shape behavior and symptoms. Avoid assuming that a child's current caregiver is the sole or primary treatment target. At the same time, trauma-informed language should not minimize current safety concerns, coercion, caregiver behavior, or the need for clear limits and accountability.
