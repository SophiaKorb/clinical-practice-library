Clinical Cross-Check

Trauma, autism, ADHD, language disorder, intellectual disability, anxiety, sleep problems, sensory overload, pain, learning difficulty, and family/system stress can produce overlapping outward behavior.

Ask what changed, when, in which settings, after what events, and under which demands. Look for developmental baseline, communication access, sensory and medical contributors, trauma reminders, avoidance patterns, and contextual reinforcement.

Do not use trauma as a catch-all explanation and do not use neurodevelopmental diagnosis to rule trauma out.
