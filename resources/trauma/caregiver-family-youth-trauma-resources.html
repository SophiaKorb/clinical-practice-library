When Families Need Treatment Rather Than Information

Psychoeducation is not a substitute for assessment or treatment when symptoms are persistent, severe, impairing, linked to ongoing danger, or accompanied by significant caregiver-child conflict. Use the Treatment Models shelf to distinguish TF-CBT, CPP, CFTSI, AF-CBT, ARC, SFCR, PCIT/CARE, PFA, and SPR according to age, timing, symptom pattern, family context, and treatment setting.
