# Privacy, copyright, and test-security boundary

This repository is intended to be shareable.

## Never commit

- protected health information
- client names, initials, dates of birth, chart numbers, contact information, or case-specific identifiers
- completed assessment forms or scored protocols
- raw test responses or item-level data
- restricted test questions, stimuli, scoring keys, manuals, or proprietary norms
- copyrighted journal PDFs unless redistribution is explicitly licensed
- unofficial copies of commercial measures

## Safe to commit when appropriately sourced

- bibliographic metadata
- links to publishers, official repositories, and legitimate public forms
- original clinical cautions and interpretive limits
- author-created handouts intended for sharing
- high-level descriptions of measures
- public-domain resources
- taxonomy, navigation, and search data

## Assessment-specific rule

Possession is not authorization. A PDF in a drive or email does not establish the right to reproduce, administer, score, convert to electronic format, or redistribute an instrument.

## Historical research

Historically influential studies may remain in the catalog with current caveats. Inclusion is not endorsement of every conclusion, method, or historical practice.
