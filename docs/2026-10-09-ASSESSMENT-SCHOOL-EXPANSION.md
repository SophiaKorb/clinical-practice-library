## Implemented in this batch

### Integrated assessment casebook

Three fictional, layered cases:

1. Longstanding developmental differences with recent peer-safety/trauma concerns: autism, ADHD, trauma/anxiety, and language/learning/medical contributors can coexist.
2. Established developmental autism plus acquired aphasia: distinguish comprehension, expressive output, cognition, fatigue, and reliable response access; do not equate more speech with adequate consent or better cognition.
3. Worksheet difficulty: compare instruction, math concepts, attention/memory load, language/output demands, feedback history, and learner perspective.

Each has a visual timeline, four competing hypothesis cells, next-evidence cautions, support plan, tool links, functional review, and advanced supervision questions. The case switcher displays one case at a time and exposes its selected state accessibly. There is no diagnostic verdict, test-score ranking, proprietary material, or client-specific information.

### Learning and access: two tracks, one plan

A two-lane visual separates teaching a skill from supporting access, then merges both into functional review. Four interactive examples show how the purpose of a task changes interpretation of read-aloud/text-to-speech, calculator, speech-to-text, and a visible step list. Modification is explicitly distinguished from accommodation. Links return to detailed original diagnosis guides and integrated cases.

### Downloadable fillable worksheets

- Learning and access plan: one landscape page, 10 fillable fields, parallel learning/access lanes and joint review.
- Competing hypotheses assessment map: one landscape page, 17 fillable fields, three hypothesis rows with supporting/contrary evidence and discriminating steps, access/safety, support, outcomes, and review.

Public blank originals, attributed once to Sophia Cohon, PhD. Created from original synthesis; no copyrighted test content reproduced. The generator is retained in the repository. Embedded fonts resolve inconsistent form rendering in the initial version.
