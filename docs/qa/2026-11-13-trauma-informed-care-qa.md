## Why trauma-informed care needs its own QA track

A library can use respectful wording without demonstrating organizational trauma-informed care. The **SAMHSA 2023** Practical Guide describes the four R assumptions, six principles (safety; trust/transparency; peer support; collaboration; empowerment/voice/choice; cultural, historical and gender considerations), and ten organizational implementation domains. It distinguishes organizational culture/policy/practice changes from merely offering trauma-specific therapies. The **AAP 2021** clinical report describes relational, developmentally appropriate child/family care, treatment referral and prevention of re-traumatization and secondary traumatic stress. An individual's reported trauma or ACE count alone does not establish PTSD, current danger, or why behavior occurred.

**CPL source text reviewed:** The trauma start-here page already explicitly separates trauma-informed practice from trauma-specific treatment; clinical guides distinguish current danger, ADHD/autism/communication and medical explanations, and caution against unsafe conjoint work. Grounding materials emphasize choice, sensory alternatives and stopping distressing exercises. These are narrow *content observations*, **not a claim that every paragraph is correct or that SAMHSA standards have been met**.

## Clinical, accessibility, rights and publication gates — all open

- **Clinical:** licensed trauma/PTSD/dissociation and child/family reviewers must verify every claim, consent and coercion safeguards, current emergency/abuse context, strengths, disability and differential diagnosis, and treatment-model limits.
- **Organizational TIC:** a website does not establish peer involvement, systemic power sharing, transparent governance, staff training, monitoring, nondiscrimination, restraint/seclusion policy, response to complaints or staff support. These are unassessed domains, not automatically technical defects.
- **Rights/source:** NCTSN and treatment developer manuals, images and translations have source-specific reuse conditions; only links and citations were inventoried. SAMHSA 2023 PDF's specific public-domain/no-fee terms do not clear other materials.
- **Accessibility:** native screen reader, alternative inputs, text/diagram reading order, large print, CVI and physical print are not validated by axe, Chrome and selectable PDF text.
- **Translation:** source language/Spanish edition, culture, readability, historical trauma and family-provider collaboration require qualified user review.
- **Release:** no PHI, merge, production deployment, public publication, copyright license or owner approval; draft PRs #21–28 and existing published site remain unchanged. Review branch's deployment block must not be copied into production unchanged.
