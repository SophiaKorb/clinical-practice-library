## Semantic corrections and deliberate restraint

- Two identical approved backpack drawings remain in the **Usually** and **Today** rows; only the actual changed activity differs.
- The earlier school illustration resembled a home/lock. It is replaced with the approved backpack art used on the First/Then Board.
- SAME no longer shares YES's checkmark; CHANGE has its own arrows.
- The choice board's movement icon depicts a running person, not footprints or shoes.
- Abstract requests such as *show me more*, *neither*, and *I don't like it* remain prominently text-led rather than being incorrectly represented by question marks or refusals.
- The Body Clues board does not use the QUIET / headphones pictogram for *too much noise*; only for the potential support *quieter space*.
- Supported Assent now explicitly differentiates verbal/nonverbal dissent, uncertainty, refusal, pause and stopping. No assumption that these pictograms prove consent or decisional capacity.
