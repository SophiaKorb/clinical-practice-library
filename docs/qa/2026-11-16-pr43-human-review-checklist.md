## Review 1 — clinical wording, youth agency and confidentiality

Mark each item only after reviewing the **exact passage** at the linked file, source context, and the acceptance criterion. A check means a named human reviewer has agreed; no human checks are implied.

- [ ] **C01: Privacy and permitted disclosures.** [Affirming clinical conversation, §3](../../resources/community/affirming-clinical-conversation.html), exact opening: “For discretionary outreach to family, school, or community providers, seek permission and document what may be shared.” It continues with mandatory-reporting and immediate-safety exceptions. **Clinical/privacy reviewer:** distinguish discretionary, legally required, and treatment-coordination disclosures; avoid claiming unconditional confidentiality to minors or blanket parental access prohibitions. Confirm local consent/record-access rules, portal visibility, safety planning and scope of authorization. **Primary legal anchors:** [HIPAA minor personal representatives (HHS)](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/personal-representatives/index.html), [AZ A.R.S. §36-2272](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/36/02272.htm) (minor mental-health treatment consent and exceptions), [AZ A.R.S. §13-3620](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/13/03620.htm) (reporting obligations). These authorities operate at different levels and should **not** be collapsed into one blanket rule. **Acceptance:** precise, nonoverpromising, trauma-informed general language approved for public audiences or narrowed further to explicit supervision/legal consultation.
- [ ] **C02: Charting and unintended disclosure.** [Affirming clinical conversation, §1 and §2](../../resources/community/affirming-clinical-conversation.html): “Invite—but never require—a chosen name, pronouns…” and “Document actual observations and the individual's reported priorities, not inferred identity or disclosure status.” **Clinician + EHR/privacy reviewer:** check that the words do not cause shared notes/portals, visit summaries, proxy accounts, school correspondence or automated outreach to disclose private information; identify what staff should ask about preferred *contexts* of use, while preserving required legal record elements. **Acceptance:** safe options and genuine patient choice; no presumption that the client's identity must be charted.
- [ ] **C03: Non-pathologizing yet clinically precise framing.** [Affirming clinical conversation, §2](../../resources/community/affirming-clinical-conversation.html): “Sexual orientation or gender identity is not a symptom, diagnosis, or a treatment target.” **Clinical reviewer:** ensure presenting complaints, co-occurring psychiatric disorders and clinically significant distress are still assessed without turning identity into a treatment target; keep person-led goals and assess immediate safety individually. **Evidence:** [CDC 2024 Supporting LGBTQ+ Youth](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-youth/lgbtq-youth/index.html); [SFSU Family Acceptance Project](https://www.sfsu.edu/departments/family-acceptance-project). **Acceptance:** no diagnostic shortcut, but no clinically consequential assessment omissions.
- [ ] **C04: Family participation and noncoercive joint sessions.** [Caregiver support, §3](../../resources/community/caregiver-support-lgbtq-youth.html): “Joint sessions should not automatically be the first step.” Also “When family conflict includes intimidation or threats, prioritize safety and individual support…” **Family clinician / safeguarding reviewer:** confirm how to screen for intimidation, offer caregiver education separately, respect relevant parental/legal participation and the youth's informed preferences without equating adult/youth accounts in situations of coercion. **Evidence:** [SFSU Family Acceptance Project](https://www.sfsu.edu/departments/family-acceptance-project), [PFLAG family resources](https://pflag.org/find-resources/). **Acceptance:** no forced disclosure, confrontation or repair; lawful, supported collaboration.
- [ ] **C05: Consent versus agency in school/case-management handoffs.** [Youth access map, §1 and §4](../../resources/community/youth-school-community-access.html): “Ask whom the young person wants involved” and “Record permission and any restrictions on further outreach.” **School/case-management/privacy reviewer:** distinguish meaningful youth participation from statutory authority to consent, records sharing, school confidentiality and mandated escalation; include interpreters/AAC access in real-world handoff. **Acceptance:** youth agency is meaningful and legal roles are not misstated.
- [ ] **C06: Current identity-document and service rules.** [Youth access map, §2 and §3](../../resources/community/youth-school-community-access.html): “verify actual state and federal issuing-authority requirements independently” and “Ask the program for current age eligibility…”. **Legal/referral reviewer:** validate each jurisdiction and provider (program, site, audience, fee, current application process) with its issuing agency/service provider **at time of use**; do not imply a named guide is a legal authority. **Acceptance:** correct referral framing, without unsupported legal step-by-step instructions.

Confirm readable, no hidden essential controls, no horizontal page-level scrolling, reachable resource links and appropriately wrapped titles. Automated tests already check 320/390/640/1280 **CSS-pixel widths**; this is related evidence, not the same test.

Automated axe pass is scoped to WCAG checks for five pages, not full WCAG conformance or subjective readability.
