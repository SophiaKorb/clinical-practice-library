### Genuine hold and language conflicts

- **MUSD** earliest preschool Child Find referral age differs across two official district pages. Record both and recommend verifying directly; do not substitute a guessed single start age.
- **California DDS** current HTML uses “birth to four years,” while a 2025 tabular document labels the provisional range “0–5 years.” Treat the *program scope* as requiring the current DDS eligibility explanation rather than assuming an eligibility cutoff at an unstated birthday.
- **TUSD vs DES AzEIP** may present different phone numbers. Follow the current DES statewide central-referral channel for a new AzEIP request; do not declare older district-listed numbers disconnected without verification.
- **Parent Aid SafeCare** headline age and *currently funded ZIP/age intake* are different layers of eligibility. Do not treat “birth–5” alone as current availability.
- **Spanish translated handouts** contain intentionally retained prior versions and identical URL aliases. Their presence is not proof of independently certified translation or clinical fidelity.
- **Authored voice/clinical language**: “En lugar de gritar” contains a humorous example about shame not completing a bedtime routine; no clinical content edited. Optional copy/translation review remains human-gated.
- Other Tucson program listings, insurance, capacity, changing contact details, and intake requirements are **not exhaustively reverified** by this targeted sample.
