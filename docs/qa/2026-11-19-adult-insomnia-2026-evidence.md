# CPL adult chronic insomnia — comparative evidence, indexing and limitations

Reviewed: **November 19, 2026**. Draft-only dependent on passing PR #93 exact source `b73dcc43150fc9f647a230599529cef13a6c76f5`. New material is **adult-only**: do not place into school-age/child-adolescent finder results.

## Gap and source selection

The 14 CPL discovery categories each have a clinical-depth guide and case-management sheet. Existing sleep modules already distinguish chronic insomnia, circadian issues, apnea and bipolar activation, and have an insomnia/CBT-I readiness topic, a sleep-loss/activation topic, and printable access plans. A generic "sleep better" worksheet would duplicate this content. One **new clinically meaningful evidence update** was missing: the **AASM April 2026 combination-treatment guideline**, which has two different conditionally graded comparators and must not be reduced to "combination recommended."

### Dated publisher/primary sources

1. [AASM combination-treatment clinical practice guideline (13 April 2026)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s44470-025-00038-8). Formal GRADE, approved by AASM board. In **adults with chronic insomnia disorder**, *CBT-I plus insomnia medication versus medication alone*: **conditionally in favor** of combination (low certainty). *CBT-I plus medication versus CBT-I alone*: **conditionally against** routine combination (low certainty). It includes a remark that early total sleep-duration preference may lead some adults to choose combination. This does not prove medication should routinely be added to CBT-I or that medication is inadvisable for everyone.
2. [AASM 2021 multicomponent behavioral treatment guideline](https://link.springer.com/article/10.5664/jcsm.8986). **Strong** multicomponent CBT-I recommendation for adult chronic insomnia. Sleep hygiene alone is not equivalent to CBT-I.
3. [VA/DoD 2025 insomnia/obstructive sleep apnea guideline](https://www.healthquality.va.gov/guidelines/CD/insomnia/I-OSA-CPG_2025-Guideline_final_20250915.pdf), January 2025: differentiates insomnia and sleep apnea, recommends offering CBT-I and weakly suggests CBT-I before medication as first line. Applicability is specific to VA/DoD adult guidance, not pediatric treatment.
4. Existing CPL sleep guide and clinical-topic resources retain the apnea/circadian, seizure, dangerous daytime sleepiness and bipolar activation considerations. This new visual is a **comparator discussion tool**, not a diagnosis, safety or medication selector.

## Age, profession, and content boundaries

The page is indexed under `Sleep conditions`, stage `Adult` only; clinician and staff/case-manager plus direct adult client educational access (exact direct-client evidence only; **no caregiver inference**). The printable sheet does not provide doses, tapering, pharmacologic algorithms, sleep restriction schedules, a CBT-I protocol or any specific patient order. Case managers can coordinate actual access and confirm completed appointments; prescribers and trained sleep clinicians are responsible for appropriate diagnosis and therapy. The new content cannot substitute for reviewing medical red flags. Explicit `noindex`; no form input, saving or transmitting personal data.
