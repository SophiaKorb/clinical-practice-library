## Primary source conflicts and cautious resolution
1. **Federal passport sex markers.** The U.S. Department of State's current official page https://travel.state.gov/en/passports/apply/unique-needs/sex-markers.html says current applications follow the November 6, 2025 Supreme Court stay of the Orr preliminary injunction and the agency issues M/F markers according to the sex-at-birth policy. STARS' community guide contains duplicated earlier legal sections and historical 2025 litigation/injunction assumptions, including an Orr attestation pathway. The directory should therefore link to the State Department as controlling current operational guidance, rather than paraphrase STARS' older steps as a reliable route. This is a status description, **not legal advice or endorsement**.
2. **Pima County court walk-in time conflict.** The Pima County court's main current public webpage https://www.sc.pima.gov/how-do-i describes Wednesday/Thursday 8–10 AM. Its older official adult name-change packet https://www.sc.pima.gov/media/dhvn4tfa/namechange_adult.pdf describes 8–9:30 AM in one section and references distinct set hearing times elsewhere. Do **not** freeze a definitive arrival cutoff; confirm directly with Pima County Civil Clerk at the currently published agency contact.
3. **Court fee figures and procedural requirements.** STARS and Fluxx community documents carry dated historic fees and filing guidance. Use current Pima court instructions, https://www.azcourts.gov/selfservicecenter/Forms/Name-Change and the county clerk; do not substitute statewide base fee tables for Pima's total local filing cost.
4. **SSA and MVD.** Separate name changes from sex marker requests. Consult https://www.ssa.gov/life-events/change-name and https://azdot.gov/mvd/services/driver-license-ID/change-your-name before any referral or step-by-step instruction. MVD says update name with SSA first. Do not imply an unsupported state-wide gender-marker rule from a community guide.
5. **Vital records.** The Arizona Department of Health Services site https://www.azdhs.gov/licensing/vital-records/index.php provides updated amendment and registry material. Do not generalize any birth-record procedure to another state or to every gender-marker scenario.
6. **Youth privacy and eligibility.** Eon's documented ages are 13–24; Pride has campus/program-specific access; Fluxx and STARS listings lack a universal age minimum on these pages. Respect youth-led decisions about disclosure and third-party involvement. Neither provider listings nor documentation guides amount to an entitlement to a particular service.
