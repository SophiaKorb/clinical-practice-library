**Evidence links and intended limitations:**

## Learning disorders

- Map: contrast. Determine whether reading words, number and mathematics, written expression, or inaccessible task demands are blocking participation. This map is neither a quick screen nor a diagnosis.
- Sources:
  - [IES WWC reading intervention, grades 4–9 (2022)](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/29)
  - [IES WWC mathematics intervention, elementary (2021)](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/26)
  - [IES WWC elementary writing guide (2012)](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/17)
- Original visual models and worksheets are **not validated protocols**. Check setting, age, clinical/school roles, medical safety, cultural context, and any guidance updates independently.

## Psychosis / bipolar

- Map: parallel. A clinical chronology of mood, sleep, unusual experiences and functioning is different from a person-led map of distress, daily participation and chosen supports. Use both without turning the diagram into a diagnosis.
- Sources:
  - [NICE CG185 Bipolar disorder recommendations, updated 2025](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg185/chapter/recommendations)
  - [NICE CG178 Psychosis and schizophrenia recommendations](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg178/chapter/recommendations)
- Original visual models and worksheets are **not validated protocols**. Check setting, age, clinical/school roles, medical safety, cultural context, and any guidance updates independently.

## Substance use

- Map: fork. A medical safety issue, a person-led goal, a treatment-access question and a privacy concern are different starting points. Support reflection without assigning an abstinence goal or a readiness stage.
- Sources:
  - [SAMHSA TIP 35 Enhancing Motivation for Change](https://www.samhsa.gov/resource/ebp/tip-35-enhancing-motivation-change-substance-use-disorder-treatment)
  - [SAMHSA motivational interviewing advisory, updated 2026](https://www.samhsa.gov/resource/recovery/using-motivational-interviewing-substance-use-disorder-treatment)
  - [SAMHSA SUD laws and 42 CFR Part 2](https://www.samhsa.gov/substance-use/treatment/statutes-regulations-guidelines)
- Original visual models and worksheets are **not validated protocols**. Check setting, age, clinical/school roles, medical safety, cultural context, and any guidance updates independently.

## Grief / bereavement

- Map: two-path. Grief has no required sequence. A person may need ritual, connection, quiet, help with responsibilities, or specialist support. Remembrance and daily living can coexist without forcing acceptance or a timetable.
- Sources:
  - [APA overview of prolonged grief disorder](https://www.psychiatry.org/patients-families/prolonged-grief-disorder/)
  - [VA National Center for PTSD: grief reactions and timelines](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/understand/related/related_grief_reactions.asp)
  - [VA: prolonged grief disorder and its treatment](https://www.ptsd.va.gov/professional/continuing_ed/prolonged_grief_tx.asp)
- Original visual models and worksheets are **not validated protocols**. Check setting, age, clinical/school roles, medical safety, cultural context, and any guidance updates independently.
